## Common Knowledge
Zaziel was the guide who led the abandoned people of Tellus down [[The Hollow]] after [[Lux Lunae]] rose. Everyone in [[Deepsteel]] knows his story. When the sky city left, the people who stayed behind were in despair. Zaziel told them that every soul still had a greater purpose, and that he was only the messenger of "the great one", chosen to lead them to a better life.

The descent was long and dangerous, and some died. Zaziel kept everyone's faith high, helped anyone in need, and was always surrounded by an inexplicable glow. At the bottom they found a lake of dull, lifeless silver. Zaziel, by then old, spoke his last words: *"With your faith I brought you here, with mine you'll go beyond what can be seen."* He fell backwards into the lake and disappeared. The lake took on new energy, and a new era began for everyone who believed.

## What Deepsteel Believes
- **He was a messenger, not a god.** He always said so. Most of the faithful hold to it.
- **His sacrifice was not in vain.** It is remembered, celebrated and repeated every time someone dies.
- **The great one** is never named. Preachers say the lake is "listening".

## The Giving *(Deepsteel's funeral rite)*
People with no children to inherit their blessing give themselves to the lake after death, as Zaziel did. It is considered the highest honour, and the community treats it as continuing his work.
- *(suggested)* People with children may also choose it, but the tradition leans toward "the childless give, the parented pass on".
- *(suggested)* The bodies are lowered with lanterns on Hollow Night.

## Appearance and Legend
- Described as always glowing faintly, "a light that came from inside".
- *(suggested)* No images of him survive. Every depiction in Deepsteel differs, and the faithful say that is deliberate.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
- Zaziel was the **first willing Convergent**. The glow was [[Gleam]] and "the great one" was the Reagents. He did not understand what was speaking through him, or he did and chose silence.
- The lake was dull because it was **dormant**, or because [[Lux Lunae]]'s Ascendancy drew on its power. His fall woke it.
- The Giving feeds the lake. Every faithful body adds biomass to a [[Gleam]] well. This is a nursery that the people are volunteering for.
- Whatever remained of him is the source of CIX's genetic sample. *(see below)*

## Link to CIX
[[CIX]]'s Project Subject:555 started from a "viable sample of Zaziel's DNA". Open options:
1. The lake returns relics: hair, bone, or something that grows back.
2. The sample comes from the lake itself, so Zaziel's genetic pattern is in the Gleam.
3. The lab found a living descendant in secret.

If option 2 is chosen, CIX is the closest thing to Zaziel the world has produced. He may or may not be the same "someone" the Reagents are waiting to use.
