## Common Knowledge
Madeline Lomazin is one of Ardentia's best-known songwriters and the wife of [[Roger Lomazin]]. Her songs have been recorded by many of the city's biggest names, including [[Dione Steels]]. Her daughter [[Luster]] first sang on one of her tracks, which needed a younger voice.

## Role
- Writes for artists at [[Glow Gleam Records]] and for her own projects.
- Her favorite of her own songs is *Where's My Love* by [[Dione Steels]], according to Luster.
- Sometimes listens to Luster's studio sessions with Roger.

## Personality
- Gentle, observant, and inclined to say less than she thinks. *(suggested)*
- Writes about longing and loss, and is most open on the page. *(suggested)*
- Protective of Luster, in a quieter way than Roger. *(suggested)*

## Relationships
- **[[Roger Lomazin]]:** husband. They trust each other's work and avoid each other's business. *(suggested)*
- **[[Luster]]:** daughter and collaborator. Madeline taught her how to find a song.
- **[[Dione Steels]]:** a long collaborator, closer to a friend than to a client. *(suggested)*

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(all suggested)*
- Madeline suspects what the label does. She has chosen not to ask Roger, and the choice weighs on her.
- Her songs seem to **work** more than they should: audiences cry together, fans describe feeling "chosen". She writes without Gleam, and she is starting to wonder whether that is why they work.
- She is the person most likely to side with Luster if shown the truth, and the person most likely to be hurt by it.

## Hooks
- Madeline quietly gives a PC a demo of a song that has never been released, and asks them to play it for Luster, away from Roger.
- A song written by Madeline gets a reaction in a crowd that PCs cannot explain.
- She asks the party to find out what happened to a singer who vanished from the label.

## Open Questions
- Has Madeline ever used Gleam, and does she know about the Sweets?
- Is the effect of her songs natural, or is it Gleam in the room?
- Who was the singer she wrote for first, and where is that person now?
