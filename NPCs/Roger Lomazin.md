## Common Knowledge
Roger Lomazin is the chairman of [[Glow Gleam Records]] and one of the most powerful people in Ardentia's music industry. He is married to the songwriter [[Madeline Lomazin]] and is the father of [[Luster]]. In public he is warm, generous and good with a crowd. Artists describe him as the man who "believes in you before you do".

## Role
- Runs [[Glow Gleam Records]]: signings, tours, and which artists get the big stages at [[Calium Arena]].
- Sits among the families and operators who hold real power in [[Ardentia]]. *(suggested)*
- Is the one who signed off on the medical decision about [[Luster]]'s arm.

## Personality
- Charming, patient, and very good at making any request sound like a favor.
- Proud of Luster in a way that is hard to separate from ownership. *(suggested)*
- Rarely angry. People become more careful when he is calm. *(suggested)*

## Relationships
- **[[Madeline Lomazin]]:** wife and creative partner. He trusts her songs and keeps the business out of her studio. *(suggested)*
- **[[Luster]]:** daughter. He listens in on her sessions and is the reason her contracts are easy.
- **[[Dione Steels]]:** an early signing of the label. *(suggested)*

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(all suggested)*
- Roger knows the label's artists are given Gleam: in the "Sweets", the studio water, the stage equipment. He considers it the price of the industry and a kindness.
- He is Anointed and has been for years. He keeps a silvered fingertip hidden under a ring.
- He believes his daughter's arm saved her life. He does not tell her it was also a way to keep her exposed.

## Hooks
- A former artist of the label tells a PC that Roger "stopped sleeping" several years ago.
- Roger invites the party to a private dinner and asks them to look after Luster.
- A rival label offers to expose him if the PCs can bring proof.

## Open Questions
- What stage is Roger at: Anointed, Silvered, or past it?
- Does he act for himself, for the label, or for something behind both?
- Does he know what Gleam is, or just what it does?
