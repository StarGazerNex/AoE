A brown hawk, given to [[Titania]] as a chick by [[Allium]] in the garden. She has flown with Tia ever since. Pipin is the first to notice the forest humming before the Seep opens, and refuses to land near it.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Pipin is old for a hawk. Allium once fed him a few drops of water from a forest spring that turned out to be the first seep. That is why he sees *through* Convergent: he screams at them, and only at them. He is the party's early-warning system.

## Hooks
- Pipin brings back a silver feather.
- He will not enter [[Evenfall]].
