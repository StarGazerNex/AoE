Harald is a twenty-two-year-old Skykin born in the floating city of Lux Lunae. He is the first son of [[Shekthar]] (50) and [[Mesli]] (45), and has a younger sister named [[Kaylie]] (19). His family occupies an unusual position within the city: his father belongs to [[Lacus Somniorum]], while his mother belongs to [[Lacus Timoris]]. Because of this, Harald grew up surrounded by two very different but equally influential ways of understanding the world.

From his father, Harald learned to value dreams, omens, celestial patterns, and the long view of history. From his mother, he learned the importance of vigilance, protection, secrecy, and the dangers that can threaten a city suspended in the sky. Rather than forcing him to choose between their perspectives, this upbringing made Harald curious about both. He became someone who wanted to understand not only how Lux Lunae survived, but why it existed in the form it did.

Harald is defined above all by his curiosity. He is deeply interested in the history of the world, the appearance of Gleam, the origins of the [[Lunarian Coven]], the mysterious mind-controlling voice associated with it, and the full truth behind the Ascendancy. He does not simply accept the stories he was raised with. He wants to investigate them, test them, and discover what his city’s leaders may be hiding.

When Harald reached the age of twenty—known in Lux Lunae as two hundred and forty-eight moons—he underwent the [[Tidal Lock]]. Like every citizen of the city, he was required to choose a Lacus, swear an oath to protect Lux Lunae, and be ritually drowned in Gleam before emerging reborn. Rather than following either of his parents into Somniorum or Timoris, Harald chose [[Lacus Solitudinis]].

His choice reflected his true interests. Solitudinis was the faction of scholars, archivists, arcana-mechanics, and keepers of forbidden knowledge. It was the branch most concerned with the city’s past, the nature of Gleam, and the ancient technology that kept Lux Lunae airborne. For Harald, it was the only faction that offered a path toward the answers he had always sought.

After completing the [[Tidal Lock]], Harald became an active member of the Lunarian Coven through [[Lacus Solitudinis]]. He also developed a rare side effect known as Astroderm. His dark purple skin became marked with metallic freckles resembling stars. According to the records kept by Solitudinis, only around five percent of the Coven develop this condition. Whether Astroderm is merely a rare magical consequence or a sign of something more significant remains unknown.

Harald’s life changed when a teacher at Plenilunium left a century-old secret tome on his desk after class. The book was called [[Lucens Liquidus]]. Though small enough to resemble a notebook, it weighed at least eleven pounds, or five kilograms. Its leather cover was worn and spent, with metallic fittings at each corner.

When Harald opened it, the Somniorum magic his father had secretly taught him at home triggered a powerful vision. He saw himself drooling Gleam, paralyzed and unable to move or speak. The vision then expanded outward: first to his family, then to his friends and acquaintances, then to the entire population of Lux Lunae, and finally to a crowd of thousands of people all suffering from the same affliction.

At the end of the vision, Harald saw the hands of the Moonarch Superior looming over these people like those of a giant puppeteer. This experience convinced him that something was deeply wrong within Lux Lunae. Rather than trusting the Coven to explain it, he decided to leave the city in secret and seek answers from a less closed source.

Harald’s central conflict is therefore not simply a desire for adventure. He is torn between his loyalty to his family, his faction, and the city that raised him, and his growing fear that Lux Lunae itself may be built upon a terrible secret. His departure is an act of curiosity, defiance, and self-preservation.
