Mog’el is a young Ribbet and a survivor of the [[Ashen-Paw]] clan’s civil war. He was raised amid violence, displacement, and the slow collapse of the traditions that had defined his people for generations. Unlike [[Harald]], whose departure from [[Lux Lunae]] was driven by curiosity and a desire to uncover hidden truths, Mog’el’s journey began with loss. His home was destroyed not by accident, but by deliberate outside interference.

As a child and young adult, Mog’el grew up on the front lines of the conflict between the [[Smoke-Bound]] traditionalists and the [[Gleam-drinkers]]. He fought to defend his clan’s heritage alongside his family and friends. His weapon was his father’s heavy iron kiseru, a pipe that connected him directly to the practices and beliefs of his ancestors. For Mog’el, the kiseru was not merely a tool; it was a symbol of continuity, discipline, and resistance.

When the [[Smoke-Bound]] made their final stand, Mog’el did not achieve a heroic victory. Instead, he helped create a brief chance for survival. He threw his heavy frame against a collapsing barricade and held the line long enough for his parents and a handful of friends to escape into the wild. His defense ultimately failed. Mog’el was beaten down, bound in heavy iron links, and captured alongside other surviving members of his clan.

Mog’el was loaded onto a Coven sky-barge ascending from a staging post upriver, destined for [[Lux Lunae]]. He had every reason to believe that he was being taken away from his home forever. Instead, the transport became the place where his fate intersected with [[Harald]]’s.

During the journey, [[Harald]]—a young scholar from Lacus Solitudinis fleeing [[Lux Lunae]]—jumped onto the sky-barge in an attempt to descend to the world below. In the confusion that followed, Mog’el overpowered a guard, slipped free of his iron bindings, and reclaimed his pipe from a cargo crate. Rather than escaping alone, he helped [[Harald]] take control of the transport and bring it down in a crash landing.

Now free, Mog’el travels with [[Harald]]. His role in the group is practical and protective. He keeps the curious Skykin safe, using his experience of war, survival, and resistance to compensate for [[Harald]]’s more academic and idealistic nature. Where [[Harald]] seeks answers, Mog’el knows what it costs to survive long enough to find them.

Mog’el continues to use his [[Gleam]]-herbal pipe, but not in the same reckless manner as the [[Gleam]]-drinkers who destroyed his clan. He uses it in measured, controlled doses, honoring the discipline taught by his elders. The smoke quiets the ringing of war in his mind, sharpens his focus, and allows him to remain connected to the divine heritage of his people.

His central conflict is whether the old ways of the [[Ashen-Paw]] can survive at all. He has lost his home, his clan’s unity, and much of his people’s future. Yet he still carries the knowledge, rituals, and responsibilities of the [[Smoke-Bound]] tradition. Mog’el may be the last living link to a way of life that [[Lux Lunae]] tried to erase.
