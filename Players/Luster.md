Luster is a twenty-three-year-old pop star from [[Ardentia]], the daughter of two of the music industry's best-known figures: songwriter [[Madeline Lomazin]] and [[Roger Lomazin]], chairman of [[Glow Gleam Records]]. She has performed solo on stage for five years, and her fans have packed [[Calium Arena]], the biggest venue in the city.

## Personality
Luster presents as warm, bubbly, and self-aware. She laughs off nerves, calls herself lucky, and openly admires her parents. Underneath that is a sharp sensitivity about being seen as a product of their influence. She defends her independence quickly ("my parents got me through the door"), and she walks out of interviews when pushed too far.

## Background
- Her first time singing was on a track her mother was helping write, which needed a younger voice. That moment set her path.
- She grew up in the industry and has known many of its people since childhood, along with its gossip and drama.
- She gave up going to college, which she wanted partly because of the coming-of-age movies she watched growing up.
- Her song *Lucky Girl Syndrome* is the one she says describes her best.
- Her favorite song written by her mother is *Where's My Love* by [[Dione Steels]].

## Career
- Solo performer for five years, with a fanbase large enough to fill [[Calium Arena]].
- She misses the early years, when the fandom was small enough that she knew many fans personally.
- Her parents sometimes listen in on her studio sessions, which she finds intimidating because of their experience.
- She says she would give up dancing before singing, because poor dancing is easier to hide than a weak voice.

## Rituals
- **Before a show:** a single Sweet, to raise her energy and calm her jitters.
- **After a show:** goes straight to the bathroom to cool down with water on her wrists and neck.

## Gleam
Luster has a robotic arm powered by [[Gleam]]. She fell from the stage to the floor during a show and landed on her arm, and the doctors said her parents' choice was this or losing it. She says she never chose it and thanks her parents for the decision.

She speaks publicly against recreational use of Gleam, and against using it to gain power. Critics call her a hypocrite, since her arm, her equipment, and her family's wealth all depend on it. She answers that the industry relies on it so heavily that it is hard to avoid, and she says she is working to minimize it in her shows.

## Controversies
- **Nepotism.** She has been asked whether her father's position at [[Glow Gleam Records]] boosted her popularity, or influenced where she plays. She gets defensive, and her answers avoid the question.
- **Gleam at the label.** She says she had never heard the rumors about Gleam consumption inside [[Glow Gleam Records]] and seemed confused by them.
- **Gleam and her voice.** An interviewer alleged she drinks Gleam to make her voice more soothing. She denied it angrily and ended the interview.
