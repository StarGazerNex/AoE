Luster: I’m Luster, 23 years old, and this is who I am.  

Interviewer: Anything that makes you uncomfortable in the studio?

Luster: Oh, having my parents listening in! They have years of experience over me and most anyone else in the rooms with me. (smiles) I love them, but it’s like driving a car next to someone who is so experienced, you feel like you might fail and disappoint at any point.

Interviewer: What dreams did you put on hold to follow this one?

Luster: Going to college, actually. Growing up watching all of those coming of age movies in college made me want to go there, but life takes its turns. (giggles)

Interviewer: What was the most memorable moment in your childhood?

Luster: The first time my parents asked me to sing, it just lit the spark in me to follow down this path. It was for a track my mom was helping write, they needed a younger voice and, well (shrugs), they chose me.

Interviewer: Talking about Your mom, [[Madeline Lomazin]]. She is a famous songwriter. What's your favorite song she ever wrote?

Luster: Definitely hard to choose, she has quite an extensive roster of songs under her belt. (with a thoughtful expression) I would probably say Where’s My Love by [[Dione Steels]]. It’s just so heartbreaking and such meaningful lyrics, one of the songs I come back to for inspiration, every time.

Interviewer: Your parents are pretty well known in the Industry. What was it like to grow up inside that world?

Luster: Fun and so chaotic! I have had relationships with people like they’re family, since I was little. I got to know a lot of scoops and also saw some drama from the inside, which wasn’t pretty. (laughs nervously) But that can happen anywhere really. 

Interviewer: You’ve been singing solo on stage for five years now. What's the best memory you have on stage?

Luster: It’s hard to pick one… (with a thoughtful face, eyes moving around, almost like searching for the memory) probably the first time I filled up the biggest venue in [[Ardentia]], [[Calium Arena]]. The energy was amazing, I couldn't ask for a better fan base. They came in force to support me.

Interviewer: What's your ritual before going on stage? And after?

Luster: UUh fun! I think the thing I do the most before going on stage is to have a single Sweet, helps me get the energy up just before entering, and it helps calm performance jitters a little bit. After I get off stage the first thing I do is go to the bathroom to cool down, like some water on the wrists and neck area.

Interviewer: Which song of yours describes you best?

Luster: Lucky Girl Syndrome! (claps once with a smile on her face, amused)  I have had a very fortunate life, my parents were in a very lucky and good position when I came into their life, there couldn't have been a better combination of conditions to have a baby at the time.

Interviewer: If you had to choose something to lose that would impact your job, what would it be?

Luster: Probably my ability to dance, I can just sing and walk around, without having to dance. I love to be able to do it all tho, I find it makes a much better show. But singing is something you can’t mask as easily, as poor dancing skills.

Interviewer: What do you miss the most from the earlier years in your career?

Luster: Getting to know my fans a lot better. (looks directly at the camera then back before continuing) The fandom was so small that I knew so many of them on an almost personal level, something I unfortunately can’t do anymore, so I let my connection to them be translated through my music.

Interviewer: On a more serious note, You speak against the consumption or direct use of [[Gleam]] in the body, but you have had Gleam fused into you. Does the hate and the calls of hypocrisy ever affect you?

Luster: Oh, because of this? (shows robotic arm powered by Gleam) I never chose for this to happen to me. My parents were forced on a hard decision, I had a pretty hard fall during a show. From the Stage directly to the floor, it could’ve been a much worse fate had I landed some other way. I landed pretty well, for someone who landed over their arm. The doctors said it was either this (shows arm again) or losing it completely. I thank my parents every day for making that decision, but recreational or the use of it to obtain power could be very dangerous. SO no, the hate comments about this don’t affect me. (visibly starting to get worked up)

Interviewer: For someone who speaks so much against Gleam, all of your equipment and parents' money are built upon it and now even you. What do you make of people calling out on THIS hypocrisy?

Luster: Well… Hum… In our world, that requires so much use of it in the technology that surrounds us (gestures towards all the equipment around them) and helps our industries be fresh and creative it’s difficult to avoid it. People call it whatever they want, I am in a place that unfortunately relies too much on the use of it, but I am working in trying to minimize the use of it on my shows.

Interviewer: Your dad, [[Roger Lomazin]], is the Chairman for the label that represents you, [[Glow Gleam Records]]. Do you think that this has influenced your popularity in the industry?

Luster: (looks shocked, taken aback by the question) …That’s quite the suggestion you’re making there… not something I would think to answer here… I don’t know how much that could influence my standings with the public.

Interviewer: You've played in a lot of big venues all over the continent. Does your dad influence the decision of where you play?

Luster: (with an annoyed face) If my dad was influencing everything in my life you probably wouldn’t be having this interview right now. 

Interviewer: They are a big part of your life. Do you think you could’ve gotten where you are now without your parents?

Luster: My parents got me through the door, but I’ve been dealing with the industry for long enough to know I’ve grown and worked with it. They might be a big part of my life and a big inspiration, but it’s not about them.

Interviewer: There are rumors going around about the consumption of gleam inside Glow Gleam Records. Have you heard about it? What’s your stand on it?

Luster: That is the first time I have heard of that. (looks confused) Like I’ve said previously, I am against the consumption and use of Gleam, as it can become quite dangerous. 

Interviewer: Along those lines we would also like to know,  What do you say to the allegations about you drinking Gleam to make your voice more soothing?

Luster: No! Hm Hm! Not happening! (takes off mic and gets up) this interview is over! That is an insult to me and my career! (goes out the door)

(Camera cuts)
