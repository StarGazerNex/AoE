## Who You Are
You are a 22-year-old Loreborne Skykin Moon Witch from the floating city of Lux Lunae, known privately as _the Descended Lunarian_. You are the first son of Shekthar (50), a seer of Lacus Somniorum, and Mesli (45), a warden of Lacus Timoris. Your sister Kaylie is 19. Your dark purple skin is marked with metallic freckles like stars, called **Astroderm**, a rare side effect of the Tidal Lock.

You left Lux Lunae in secret, carrying a heavy tome a teacher left on your desk. You jumped onto a Coven sky-barge to descend to the world below, and in the chaos you helped a captive Ribbet named Mog'el take the barge and crash it. You travel with him now, and he keeps you safe.

## Two Upbringings
You grew up between two ways of seeing the world, and you were never made to choose.

- **From your father** you learned to value dreams, omens, celestial patterns and the long view of history. He also taught you dream magic in secret.
- **From your mother** you learned vigilance, protection, secrecy and the dangers that threaten a city suspended in the sky.

It made you curious about both. You wanted to understand not only how Lux Lunae survived, but why it exists in the form it does. You are interested in the history of the world, the appearance of Gleam, the origins of the Lunarian Coven, the mysterious mind-controlling voice associated with it, and the full truth behind the Ascendancy. You do not simply accept the stories you were raised with. You want to test them.

## Lux Lunae and the Coven
This is what you were raised on.

- **The city.** Lux Lunae was once part of the older city of Tellus. In the Ascendancy it tore free and rose into the sky. The part that stayed behind is **The Hollow**.
- **The Coven.** It governs the city and is intensely secretive. It treats information as a way to manipulate and influence, and it shapes world politics from the shadows. The world believes it can see the future.
- **The four Lacus.**
    - **Somniorum** are visionaries, seers and oracle-witches. They read stars and moons, dream-walk and scry. They predict atmospheric changes, planar tears and threats, settle high-level disputes and shape long-term policy.
    - **Veris** are healers, alchemists and growth witches. They keep the suspended gardens, crop terraces and clean rainfall, and run the sanctuaries and public health.
    - **Timoris** are wardens, combat witches and shadow-weavers. They keep the wards, cloaking illusions and air-defence barriers against dragon raids, sky pirates and rival nations. They also root out corruption and dangerous magic inside the city.
    - **Solitudinis** are scholars, archivists and arcana-mechanics. They keep sky-tomes, celestial maps and forbidden artefacts, and they maintain the leyline engines and skystone foundations that keep the city from falling.
- **The Council** has five seats. A witch from each Lacus holds four. The fifth belongs to the **Moonarch Superior**, who appears only in a black veil. Nobody knows the Moonarch's face, or why they hide it.
- **Education** runs through Novilunium (elementary), Corniculata (high school) and Plenilunium (university). It prepares every citizen for the day they choose a Lacus.
- **Ancestries.** The city's six main ancestries are Aetheris, Elves, Skykin, Humans, Faerie and Fungrils.

## The Tidal Lock and Your Choice
At twenty, which is 248 moons by Lunarian reckoning, every citizen must choose a Lacus, swear an oath to protect the city, and be drowned in Gleam. They emerge reborn. It is a coming-of-age ceremony and a binding act of loyalty. A citizen who has not done it cannot hold a seat in a Lacus.

You did it. You did not follow either parent. You chose **Lacus Solitudinis**, because it was the faction closest to the city's past, the nature of Gleam and the ancient technology that keeps Lux Lunae airborne. It was the only path that seemed to lead to your questions.

Most people feel nothing unusual afterward beyond a lasting calm and a sense of belonging. You developed Astroderm. Solitudinis's records say about five percent of the Coven do. Whether it is a rare magical consequence or a sign of something more, nobody has told you.

## Gleam: What You Were Taught
The Coven teaches that Gleam is a source of power and prophecy. It says Lux Lunae's Ascendancy was possible because the city understood Gleam so early after it appeared, and that the same understanding is why the world thinks the city can see the future. Solitudinis maintains the Gleam technology that keeps the city afloat. Refined Gleam is believed to be safer than raw, because it slows the effects.

You have no proof for any of this beyond what you were told, and you have started to doubt it.

## Gleam: What Everyone Knows
- Gleam is a heavy silver liquid metal that wells up from pools. It does not glow or hum, but standing near one gives a faint sense of being noticed.
- The oldest records describe a single pool in the mountains. Within a generation there were dozens, and the number keeps growing. Wells open without warning and never in the same kind of place twice.
- It heals wounds in days, ripens crops early, makes the old stand straighter, and makes metal hold an edge forever.
- Drinking erases fatigue. Bathing heightens the senses and builds muscle quickly. Anointing leaves a visible sheen.
- Left alone, a well rises and spreads. Grass, trees and animals thin near an old well even as the people nearby thrive.
- People who use too much sometimes "burn out" and are lost. The popular explanation is overdose. Many become addicted, or dependent on one particular well. Some people resist change better than others, and nobody knows why.

## The Wellcities and the Dry
- A **wellcity** is a settlement that grew around a well. They are the richest places in the world and are growing faster than any city in living memory. To their people the well is a sacred gift.
- Residents are sorted by how deeply they have immersed: **Dim** are casual users, **Anointed** are regular bathers with a visible sheen and heightened gifts, and **Convergent** are those who have transformed.
- **Convergence** is called a willing transformation into something more than human. It starts small: a silvered fingertip, a reflection that lingers a second too long, a person who no longer needs to sleep. The faithful treat it as ascension, and many Convergent hold leadership roles. Many are kind.
- **Ardentia** is the best known wellcity, a former fishing town on the west coast. It is famous for its arenas and casinos, where anyone can become a legend or lose everything.
- Wellcities trade Gleam-forged weapons, tools and medicine to fund their growth. They buy technology from Deepsteel.
- The **Dry** refuse Gleam. Some seal wells with stone and salt, some burn wellcity supply lines, and some move away. They are not simply heroes. Some are right to be afraid, some are zealots, and burning a well also abandons the people who depend on it.

## The Wider World
- **The Hollow and Deepsteel.** The crater Lux Lunae left behind now holds a city called Deepsteel. Its people answered the loss with iron, steam and wire. It prizes open schematics, guilds and apprenticeship, the opposite of the Coven's hoarded secrets. It officially distrusts Gleam, and its inventions are spreading. Horseless carriages now run on roads leading out of it.
- **Zaziel.** You have heard the story told in Deepsteel. After the sky city rose, he led the abandoned people down into the Hollow, told them he was only the messenger of "the great one", and fell into a silver lake at the bottom. The lake is holy to the faithful.
- **The Ashen-Paw and the Smoked Plains.** The Ashen-Paw were a nomadic clan of artisans, storytellers and herbalists in the far north-east, downriver of Lux Lunae along the Ashwater. Mog'el has told you they fell into a civil war that scattered his people.

## What You Never Understood
These are the questions you have carried your whole life:

- What caused the Ascendancy, and why only part of Tellus rose.
- What became of The Hollow.
- The true origin of Gleam.
- The identity of the Moonarch Superior.
- The nature of the mind-controlling voice associated with the Coven.
- What Astroderm actually is.

## Why You Left
A teacher at Plenilunium left a century-old tome on your desk after class. It is called _**Lucens Liquidus**_. It is small enough to resemble a notebook, but it weighs at least eleven pounds. Its leather cover is worn, and it has metal fittings at each corner.

When you opened it, the dream magic your father secretly taught you triggered a vision. You saw yourself drooling Gleam, paralysed and unable to move or speak. The vision widened: to your family, your friends and acquaintances, the whole population of Lux Lunae, and finally a crowd of thousands, all suffering the same way. At the end you saw the hands of the Moonarch Superior over these people, like a giant puppeteer's.

You decided something was deeply wrong in Lux Lunae. You did not trust the Coven to explain it. You left in secret to look for answers from a less closed source.

## What You Carry
- _**Lucens Liquidus**_, the tome from your desk.
- Your Astroderm and your Moon Witch training.
- The memory of a vision you cannot explain.
- A question you cannot put down: is the city you love built on a terrible secret?

## People You Know
- **Mog'el**: a young Ribbet and survivor of the Ashen-Paw civil war. You helped him take a Coven sky-barge and crash it. He is practical and protective, and he knows what it costs to survive long enough to find answers.
- **Shekthar** (father): taught you dream magic. Somniorum.
- **Mesli** (mother): taught you vigilance. Timoris. Timoris is the arm of the Coven that would stop a defector.
- **Kaylie** (sister, 19): has not yet chosen a Lacus.
- **Visindi** (22): your oldest friend, since Novilunium. Impulsive, a troublemaker, the one who showed you that questioning rules can lead somewhere better. Chose Timoris.
- **Sunna** (23): your childhood love, a prodigy who has had visions and spoken with spirits since she was a baby. She defended you from bullies when you both were six, and probably still fights better than you. Chose Somniorum.
