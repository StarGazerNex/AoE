Nymestria is a Batkin raised in a [[Gleam]]-worshipping sanctum, where she was trained from childhood as an assassin and groomed to become the next Sacrifice. She was never a willing believer. She was shaped into one, and the story of how she stopped being one is the core of her character.

She grew up in an orphanage that doubled as a training ground, with classes, combat instruction, and a quiet system of favoritism. Her mentor, [[Ambriel]], singled her out early. Among the older girls she met [[Dahlia]], the previous Sacrifice, whose fate would later haunt her.

Her first test was her first kill. Afterward Ambriel taught her that the god wanted it so, and that she acted in the god's stead. When she questioned later kills, she was brainwashed, and perhaps beaten, back into obedience. She killed so much that she began to dissociate, and she would only come back to herself while washing blood from her skin. Members of the rebellion sometimes begged her to come to her senses when she was lost in a kill. It never worked then, but their words stayed with her.

## Backstory

**Graduation.** Before the graduation and selection, Ambriel told her she would be the chosen one. Her first reaction showed doubt, and his anger at it. She also learned what happens to those who aren't chosen: they become guards, servants, or are abandoned. She had to sit through her peers' anxiety while already knowing the outcome. After the graduation came a coronation, with her veiled. Her life changed completely. Attendants bathed her, her bath moved to a gold Gleam bathing room, and she was celebrated for a month with feasts and balls. At the masked ball, a rebellion infiltrator danced with her and, in that moment, replayed memories of rebels she had killed.

**The ritual.** In the nights before, fasting and dreams fed her growing doubt. On the night itself she was dressed and adorned, then taken to the well, where an altar stands in the middle and people chant around it. The first part was a "cleansing." In the second she was chained kneeling and lashed, and the gleamblood entered her system like poison, with agonizing pain. The memories of previous sacrifices flooded her weakened mind, mixed with the memories the infiltrator had shown her, and she saw Dahlia. Fear, stress, and something like ascension, perhaps with help from an invader, broke her chains. She tried to fly, but her wings had been destroyed in the lashing.

**The run.** She fled in a bloodied gown and veil, hiding in shadow, now hunted by the vampires and the girls she grew up with. She had once hunted the rebellion the same way. The memory of Ambriel's face, full of anger, disappointment, and hurt, made her doubt her own senses. She passed the statue of the "savior" on her way out of town. When the vampire who used to harass her nearly caught her, a rebel saved her, and her scythe came back to her through a guard on the rebel side. She reached the outskirts.

**Crystalight.** The rebellion presented her to the group. Some saw a chance to topple the sanctuaryum, some hated her for what she had done and the people she had killed, and some were glad to have one of the deadliest weapons on their side. They also showed her what the sanctum actually does, and what Gleam can do: advanced technology built from smuggled and scarce resources, including radios, vehicles, small devices, and weapons. Her veil and mask were repurposed as armor and bullets, and she converted her scythe into a rifle. She spent two months bonding with them, overhearing debates about whether she should stay, what would happen if the sanctum caught her again, or whether to send her "upstairs" and hope she survived. They gave her a makeover so she would be harder to recognize.

**Busted.** The rebellion's location was revealed, possibly by someone she had once arrested instead of killing, who had been tortured for years. The rebels rushed to smuggle her out. While they fought, she fled with a Tidekin guide who knew the way, and saw Ambriel fly over the battle in full armor, angrier than she had ever seen him, before she lost sight of him.

**The escape.** They ran for days or weeks through dark, dangerous corridors until they reached a lake. She had to swim it underwater, because "if it was easy, anyone would do it." The Tidekin guided her, helped her breathe, and fought the monsters in the water. On the far side he had to turn back to help the rebellion, and he left her instructions, perhaps passed to her by a mushroom witch who turned her memories and directions into instincts. After that came months of running, fighting, and little rest through giant spiders and larvae, walls of screaming dead, undead, and ghosts, in the manner of an epic underworld. At the exit, a hydra guarded the way. Every head she cut grew back as two, and it caught part of her wing. She cut another head to slow her fall, ran for the edge, and leapt, flying before she hit the waves and rocks.

**The new beginning.** The first thing she saw outside was blue: waves and the night sky. She flew hurt for hours with no destination, passed out from exhaustion, and fell like a comet. She woke with the sun on her face for the first time in her life, in the foliage of a tree.

## Central conflict
Nymestria was made into a weapon and a sacrifice by people who told her it was divine will. She must decide who she is once that is gone, and what she owes the rebels, the people she killed, and the girls she left behind.
