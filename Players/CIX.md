Log #05662 
There's more to this project than I expected. His sense of hearing appears to be vastly better than expected since not only reacts to the different sounds and voices, but can also distinguish and have different reactions depending on the voices. For example , when the rest of the lab crew talks close to the subjects pod, he shows signs of restlessness and higher BPM values. However, when I'm the one talking, the values show some reduction, it was also taken note that on the calmer days where I sing we get the lowest readings in BPM. In conclusion, we hypothesize that there might be a subconscious connection to my voice as if I, [[Jophie]], am sort his of maternal figure.
