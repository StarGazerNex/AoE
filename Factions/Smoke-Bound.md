The Smoke-Bound are the traditionalist half of the [[Ashen-Paw]]. They practice a disciplined relationship with the well: dried crust from its edges is blended with mountain herbs and smoked through heavy iron [[Kiseru]]. In measured pulls the smoke grants calm, steady focus, a stronger body, and sharper tactical instincts. In deeper ritual doses it grants elders guiding visions for planting, settling disputes, and honoring the gods. Their creed is that strength comes from respecting [[Gleam]]'s limits, not from consuming it. They lost the civil war and are now driven underground or into the hills.

**Rules of the tradition** - Smoke is shared at the hearth, never alone and never for pleasure. - The one who smokes names what they are smoking for. - Visions are spoken aloud to the elders. Hidden visions are a warning sign.
**Why they matter to the campaign:** their method may be the one proven way to use [[Gleam]] without [[Convergence]], and the Coven wanted it erased for that reason.
