**Role:** scholars, archivists, arcana-mechanics, and keepers of deep secrets.
**Duties:** preserving sky-tomes, celestial maps, and forbidden artifacts, and maintaining the [[Gleam]] technology that keeps [[Lux Lunae]] airborne: the leyline engines and skystone foundations. **Members:** [[Harald]]. Their records say about five percent of the Coven develops Astroderm.
**Secret:** Solitudinis holds the ancient technology but may not understand it. The tome _Lucens Liquidus_ was left on Harald's desk by someone inside, so there's a faction within this faction with its own agenda.
**If Harald vanishes:** they need to know what he took, and what he saw.
