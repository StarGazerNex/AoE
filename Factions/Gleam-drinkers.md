The Gleam-drinkers are the half of the [[Ashen-Paw]] who embraced raw [[Gleam]] and Coven-backed weaponry. They believed power should be taken directly rather than moderated through ritual. The [[Lunarian Coven]] supplied them in secret with unrefined [[Gleam]] and silver-infused steel through hidden emissaries. They won the civil war and now hold the [[Smoked Plains]].

**The tragedy:** many believe they chose freedom. In practice they traded one leash for another, since the Coven now holds their supply. Their [[Convergence]] is likely well underway.
**Hook:** some of them know they were used, and some may be willing to say so.
