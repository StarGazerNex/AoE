**Role:** visionaries, seers, oracle-witches, strategists, and spiritual guides of [[Lux Lunae]].
**Methods:** star and moon readings, dream-walking, scrying.
**Duties:** predicting atmospheric changes, planar tears, and threats to the island, resolving high-level disputes, and shaping long-term policy. Somniorum is the reason the world thinks the city can see the future.
**Members:** Harald's father [[Shekthar]].
**Secret:** their foresight is partly built on information gathered by others, including spymasters sent into the [[Smoked Plains]].
**If Harald vanishes:** they will want to read his dreams. Shekthar taught him dream magic in secret, so that links Harald directly to what they investigate.
