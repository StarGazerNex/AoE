The Ashen-Paw are a nomadic clan of the [[Smoked Plains]], once renowned as master artisans, storytellers, and deep-soil herbalists. Their culture was built on oral tradition and communal hearth-gatherings, where elders passed down history through music, story, and ritual. Their ironwork was made to last generations. Their herbalists read the rivers, soil, and mountain weather. They lived in rhythm with the land long before [[Gleam]] touched it. When a silver well opened in their territory, the elders approached it with caution rather than worship. [[Lux Lunae]] noticed, and the clan split into the [[Smoke-Bound]] and the [[Gleam-drinkers]]. Their civil war ended with the clan scattered, captured, or dead.

**Survivors:** [[Mog'el]] and an unknown number of others, some of them prisoners bound for [[Lux Lunae]].
