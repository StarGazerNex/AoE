**Role:** wardens, combat witches, and shadow-weavers.
**Duties:** the wards, cloaking illusions, and air-defense barriers against dragon raids, sky pirates, and rival nations, plus internal order, rooting out corruption and dangerous magic.
**Members:** Harald's mother [[Mesli]].
**Secret:** the same wards that keep enemies out keep citizens in, and Timoris is the arm that would quietly stop a defector.
**If Harald vanishes:** Timoris hunts him, and his own mother may be the one sent.
