## Common Knowledge
Glow Gleam Records is one of the largest labels in [[Ardentia]], chaired by [[Roger Lomazin]]. It signs singers, songwriters and performers, produces their records, and books the biggest stages in the city, including [[Calium Arena]]. It is one of the media houses that hold real power in a [[Wellcities|wellcity]].

[[Luster]] is its best-known artist. [[Dione Steels]] and [[Madeline Lomazin]] are among the names tied to it.

## What It Does *(suggested)*
- **Signings.** Scouts watch the old town and the street stages for anyone who can capture attention.
- **Studios.** Gleam-run equipment gives records a clarity other labels cannot match.
- **Tours.** The label decides which artists get a night at [[Calium Arena]] and which get the opening slot.
- **Image.** Publicists keep artists likable and keep rumors small.

## Public Image
Glow Gleam Records presents itself as a family: warm, generous, and loyal to its artists. Many of its artists stay for life. Rumors about Gleam consumption inside the label have circulated for years, and the label denies them.

## Internal Culture *(suggested)*
- Artists get a "welcome kit" on signing. It includes a box of Sweets.
- Studio water, stage lighting and monitors are Gleam-run, and no one is told which.
- Leaving the label is possible, but artists who do tend to disappear from the charts.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(all suggested)*
- The label is a **delivery system**. The Sweets, the studios and the stages expose every artist and every fan, and the label has learned that exposed audiences come back.
- A sold-out show at [[Calium Arena]] is a way of putting tens of thousands of people near a [[Gleam]]-run stage at the same time.
- Roger Lomazin knows. Whether he is also taking orders is unknown.
- The label's biggest voices are quietly Anointed. [[Dione Steels]] is the oldest example.

## Hooks
