No one knows when the [[Gleam]] first surfaced. The oldest records describe a single silver pool in the mountains, treated as a curiosity. Within a generation there were dozens of wells across the known world. They appear without warning, never in the same kind of place twice, and each one seems to grow, slowly rising and spreading if it is left alone. People who settle near a well see changes quickly. Wounds heal in days. Crops ripen early. Old people stand up straighter. Metalwork shaped with the [[Gleam]] holds an edge that never dulls. Settlements around wells become [[Wellcities]], and because the [[Gleam]] rewards those who build with it, they are growing faster than any city in living memory. **The [[Gleam]]** is also a drug, a doctrine, and a danger. Wellcity residents who simply live near it are mostly fine. The ones who immerse themselves, bathing, drinking, and being anointed, experience a euphoria they describe as feeling truly alive. Over months and years, they change. Their skin takes on a metallic sheen. Their senses sharpen, then shift. Eventually they undergo what the faithful call **[[Convergence]]**, a final, willing transformation into something that is no longer quite human, and in their own minds, no longer incomplete.
Meanwhile, the **Dry**, communities that refuse the [[Gleam]], treat the wells as omens. Some seal them with stone and salt. Some burn the [[Wellcities]]' supply lines. Some simply move away, but the wells keep opening.
