## Common Knowledge

Gleam is a liquid metal, silver and heavy like mercury, that wells up from pools no one can explain. It does not glow or hum, but anyone standing near a pool feels a faint, hard-to-name aura, the sense of being near something that is aware of them. No one knows when it first surfaced. The oldest records describe a single pool in the mountains, treated as a curiosity. Within a generation there were dozens, and the number keeps growing.

Its benefits are so large they seem impossible. Wounds close in days, crops ripen early, the old stand straighter, and metal worked with it never loses its edge. Settlements that form around a well become [[Wellcities]] and grow faster than any city in living memory. Even [[Lux Lunae]] owes its Ascendancy to Gleam, and the world credits its seers to it as well.

Most people believe Gleam is a miracle with an unfortunate side effect: those who overuse it sometimes "burn out" and are lost. The popular explanation is overdose, like any strong drug.

## Physical Properties

- **Appearance:** a silver liquid metal, mercury-like in weight and sheen.
- **Aura:** faint, mysterious, and never fully described the same way twice. People report being watched, welcomed, or recognised.
- **Crust:** the edges of a well form a mineral crust. The [[Smoke-Bound]] dry it and blend it with mountain herbs to make a silver-tinged leaf for the [[Kiseru]].
- **Forged goods:** metal worked with Gleam holds an edge forever. Weapons, tools, and medicine made this way are the best in the world, and [[Wellcities]] trade them to fund their growth. Carrying one means carrying a little of the Gleam with you.
- **Spread:** in the body or in a pool, Gleam expands slowly and steadily.

## The Wells

- Wells open without warning and never in the same kind of place twice: mountains, plains, [[Ardentia]]'s old fishing town, the [[Smoked Plains]].
- Every well grows. Left alone, it rises and spreads by consuming the life around it, so grass, trees, and animals near an old well thin out even as the people nearby flourish.
- Wildlife sometimes drinks from or is consumed by a well and takes control of it, expanding it from there. *(suggested: these wells grow faster and unpredictably, and the animals show early signs of [[Convergence]].)*
- The **Dry** seal wells with stone and salt. *(suggested: this slows a well but never kills it, and sealed wells keep slowly regrowing.)*
- Each well has a slow clock. The GM can advance it while the party is away or inactive.

## Effects by Method

| Method | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Drinking** | Erases fatigue and restores energy. |
| **Bathing** | Heightens the senses and builds muscle quickly. |
| **Anointing** | Visible sheen, sharpened gifts. This is how the *Anointed* got their name. |
| **Smoking** (Smoke-Bound method) | Calm focus, a stronger body, sharper tactical instincts, and ritual visions in deep doses. |
| **Tidal Lock** | Full immersion at age twenty, binding a citizen to [[Lux Lunae]]. |
| **Forged goods** | A constant trickle of exposure to the carrier. |

Whatever the method, the Gleam inside a person slowly expands. It is *very* slow, but it never stops.

**Refined Gleam:** the Coven and the [[Wellcities]] believe refining slows the effects. *In truth it is a placebo.* See GM Truth.

## Technology and Magic

- Magic already existed before Gleam. Gleam did not create magic, but it made magic evolve far faster than the world could absorb.
- Gleam boosts magic. Spells, rituals, and magical items near it are stronger.
- Gleam makes machines run at their absolute peak: more efficient, more powerful, with better fuel use. It is what keeps [[Lux Lunae]]'s leyline engines and skystone foundations running.
- [[Deepsteel]] officially distrusts it, but its engineers know what combining Gleam with machinery does. The temptation is the city's quiet crisis.

## The Cost

- **Addiction:** many users become addicted.
- **Dependence:** many become dependent on Gleam, or on one particular well.
- **Convergence:** see [[Convergence]]. To the public it is a willing transformation into something more than human. It begins with a silvered fingertip, a lingering reflection, a person who no longer sleeps.
- **Ancestry:** the effects vary by ancestry. *(suggested: Fungrils and Faerie react to it differently from Humans and Elves; the [[Wellcities]] have noticed but don't understand why.)*
- **Resilience:** some people resist change better than others. No one knows why.

## GM Truth

> [!warning] GM only

**What Gleam is.** Gleam is a seed. Each well was planted by the Reagents of an extra-planar species (name TBD), a dying biomechanical civilisation that is using this world as an incubator to regrow its population. The wells are nurseries. The miracle drug is bait. The healing, the fertility, and the euphoria all exist to make the world *want* more.

**The wells.** The wells slowly consume life around them to expand, and that is the point. A well is gathering biomass for something that will eventually hatch.

**Convergence.** Convergence is not a transformation, it is a takeover. A Convergent person has lost themselves, and the Reagents are puppeting the body, like a permanent episode of psychosis with someone else at the controls. The faithful call it enlightenment because the personality that remains seems happy, but the person who started the journey is gone. The "kind Convergent leaders" in the [[Wellcities]] are kind because they have learned what kindness gets them.

**Overuse.** Overusing Gleam means losing control of your mind and body to the Reagents. The world thinks it is overdose.

**Refined Gleam.** Refining does nothing. It looks purer and feels safer, and the placebo effect does the rest. Selling "refined" Gleam is therefore the safest way to keep people exposed.

**Dose and method.** The delivery method changes what the user feels but not the underlying process. Every method plants more Gleam in the body and lets it expand.

**Harald's vision.** [[Harald]]'s vision in [[Lucens Liquidus]] was not a prophecy. It was the *intention* of the Gleam already inside him: everyone drooling and paralysed while the Moonarch's hands pulled the strings. It showed him what the Gleam wanted for the world.

**The Tidal Lock.** Citizens of [[Lux Lunae]] are drowned in Gleam at twenty. Most are lucky or resilient and don't progress. The Coven believes the ritual binds citizens to the city. In truth it is a mass seeding. Why most survive it is unclear.

**Astroderm.** [[Harald]] is an exception: he manifested all the Gleam into his skin as Astroderm instead of letting it take his mind. His starry freckles are the Gleam, held at the surface instead of expanding inward.

**Reversal.** No one knows of any way to stop or reverse Convergence. *(See open question on the Smoke-Bound method.)*

**The Coven's knowledge.** The [[Lunarian Coven]] believes many things about Gleam, and most are wrong or have been steered by the Reagents. The Coven's beliefs about its own history, its foresight, and the Moonarch may all rest on manipulated information.

## Hooks

- A well opens inside a city that has never had one.
- An animal that took over a well is growing it faster than anyone can seal it.
- A Convergent leader says something only the player's missing sibling would know.
- A Deepsteel engineer shows a machine that runs forever and asks the party not to tell anyone.
- A merchant sells "refined" Gleam to the Dry at triple the price.
