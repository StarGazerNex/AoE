## Common Knowledge
Convergence is the final, willing transformation of a long-term [[Gleam]] user into something the faithful call complete. Residents of the [[Wellcities]] treat it as ascension. Outsiders call it burnout or worse. Many Convergent hold leadership roles, and many are kind.

## The Stages (a gradient, not a switch)
| Stage      | Sign                                      | Table moment                             |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Exposed    | Lives near a well or carries forged goods | None. This is most people.               |
| Anointed   | Faint sheen on the skin, sharper gifts    | Others notice before they do.            |
| Silvered   | A silvered fingertip or nail bed          | Easy to hide, hard to forget.            |
| Echo       | Reflections linger a second too long      | Mirrors, still water, polished steel.    |
| Sleepless  | No longer needs sleep, appetite fades     | Friends notice the gaps in conversation. |
| Convergent | Fully transformed, calm and certain       | The person they knew is "happier".       |

Treat each stage as a character moment first and a mechanical penalty second.

## Who Is Affected
- Speed varies by method, dose and ancestry. [[Wellcities]] have noticed but don't understand why.
- Some people resist better than others. No one knows why.
- [[Gleam-drinkers]] are likely well along.
- Refined Gleam is believed to slow it.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
- Convergence is a **takeover**. The Reagents puppet the body once the original person is gone. The remaining personality seems happy and is not the person who began.
- Kind Convergent leaders are kind because kindness has proven useful.
- Refined Gleam does nothing against it.
- **Astroderm** is the known exception: the Gleam held at the surface instead of spreading inward.
- No known reversal. The [[Smoke-Bound]] method may be a path to using Gleam without Convergence.

## Tells for the Table
- Knows something only the missing sibling would know.
- Never blinks at the right moment, or never sleeps through a long scene.
- Repeats phrases other Convergent have used.
