## Common Knowledge
A wellcity is a settlement that grew around a [[Gleam]] well. Residents heal quickly, crops ripen early, and metal holds an edge forever, so wellcities expand faster than any city in living memory. They are the richest places in the world and they know it. To their residents, the well is a sacred gift. To outsiders, it is a bargain with a price nobody states out loud.

[[Ardentia]] is the best-known example. It was a fishing town until its well opened, and within a few years it became one of the continent's great cities.

## The Three Tiers
Residents are sorted by how deeply they have immersed, not by birth.

| Tier | Who | Marks | Social position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dim** | Casual users: they live near the well, carry forged goods, drink on feast days | None visible | The majority. Treated as "not yet", with polite pity. |
| **Anointed** | Regular bathers | Visible sheen, heightened gifts | Professionals, performers, officials. The aspirational class. |
| **Convergent** | Those who have [[Convergence\|converged]] | Varies by stage | Many hold leadership roles. Many are kind. Treated with reverence. |

- **Tier and wealth overlap, but don't match.** Immersion costs money (private baths, anointing fees), so the rich tend to be Anointed. Some poor residents bathe far beyond what they can afford in exchange for a debt or a job, so they are Anointed and still live in the old districts. *(suggested)*
- **Social mobility runs one way.** Nobody becomes Dim again. People who stop using Gleam are called "dried out" and are treated with suspicion by both sides. *(suggested)*
- **Convergence is promotion, not death.** Families celebrate it. Leaving a Convergent family member's "ascension" uncelebrated is a social offense.

## Governance
Each wellcity governs itself differently, but they share three structures. *(suggested)*

- **The Well Charter.** A founding document that grants the city rights over its well. Whoever controls the Charter controls the city.
- **Wardens of the Well.** An elected or appointed body that handles access, safety, and anointing schedules. In older wellcities, Convergent members hold the senior seats.
- **Guild Houses.** Trade bodies that control the production of [[Gleam]]-forged goods.

**Ardentia as the example:** power sits with the families and investors who bought the land early, the arena and casino operators, and the label and media houses such as [[Glow Gleam Records]]. Formal government is real but is mostly a referee between them.

## Trade and Economy
- **Forged goods** (weapons, tools, medicine) are the major export. They are the best in the world, and wellcities sell them to fund their own growth.
- **Carriers carry the well.** Anyone who owns a forged item is exposed a little. Wellcities treat this as free advertising.
- **Refined Gleam** is sold as the safe option. The Coven and the wellcities believe it slows the effects.
- **Machinery.** Gleam-run machines work at peak efficiency. Wellcities buy technology from [[Deepsteel]] and put it to use, which makes Deepsteel's official distrust awkward.
- **Land and food.** The soil around a well is unusually fertile, which is why [[Ardentia]] attracted distant property owners.

## Relationship With the Rest of the World
- **The Dry** seal wells, burn supply lines, and sometimes burn wellcities. Wellcities respond with private security and with propaganda that describes the Dry as fearful and backward.
- **[[Lux Lunae]]** supplies seers, technology, and policy, and keeps its own Gleam knowledge secret. Wellcities consult the Coven's forecasts but don't know how its power works.
- **[[Deepsteel]]** is a trading partner and a quiet worry.
- **The [[Smoked Plains]]** are an example of what happens when a well is not turned into a city.

## Life by Tier: Table Moments
- A Dim shopkeeper is quietly told her business will do better if she bathes before the season opens.
- An Anointed performer hides a silvered fingertip behind a glove for a show.
- A Convergent magistrate settles a dispute with unnerving gentleness and never seems tired.
- A "dried out" former neighbour begs a PC to take a message to their family. The family won't open the door.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
- Every wellcity is a **nursery**. The city feeds the well. Population, wealth, and attention all increase its growth.
- **Convergent leaders** are kind because the Reagents have learned that kindness keeps people calm and in place. Whether they coordinate across cities is unknown.
- **Growth is the product.** A wellcity that stops growing is a well that has stopped being tended, so some Convergent officials work hard to keep the city booming.
- **Refined Gleam** is a placebo. Selling it is the safest way to keep the population exposed.
- **Wellcity expansion** is the surface expression of the well's clock. A boom might actually be a well advancing toward something.

## Hooks
- A wellcity's growth stalls, and a Convergent official quietly arranges a festival of new anointings to restart it.
- A dried-out resident offers to trade the location of a hidden well for a way out of the city.
- A Dry group asks the PCs to guide them through a wellcity to a supply line.
- A guild house wants to hire a PC to carry a forged blade across the continent and report back on how they feel afterward.
