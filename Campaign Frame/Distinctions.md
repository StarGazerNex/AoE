**The wells are everywhere, and they're growing.** Every region has a well, or soon will. There is no place far enough away to ignore the problem forever. Each well has a slow clock that the GM can advance when the party is away or inactive.
**The [[Gleam]] is not evil and not safe.** It heals. It also changes you. A character who uses it to save a dying friend has made a real, defensible choice, and a real, lasting one.
**[[Wellcities]] have their own class system.** Residents are sorted by how deeply they've immersed: the _Dim_(casual users), the _Anointed_(regular bathers, with visible sheen and heightened gifts), and the _Convergent_(those who have transformed). Many Convergent hold leadership roles. Many are kind.
**The Dry are not simply the good guys.** Some are right to be afraid, some are zealots, and a few are using the fear to seize power of their own. Burning a well also means abandoning the people who depend on it.
**[[Convergence]] is a gradient, not a switch.** Transformation shows up in small, specific ways first: a silvered fingertip, a reflection that lingers a second too long in a mirror, a person who no longer needs to sleep. The GM should treat these as character moments, not just penalties.
**[[Gleam]]-forged goods are valuable and contested**. Weapons, tools, and medicine made with [[Gleam]] are the best in the world, and [[Wellcities]] trade them to fund their growth. Carrying one means carrying a little of the [[Gleam]] with you.
