## Common Knowledge
The Tidal Lock is the coming-of-age rite of [[Lux Lunae]]. At the age of twenty, or 248 moons by Lunarian reckoning, every citizen must choose a Lacus, swear an oath to protect the floating city, and be drowned in [[Gleam]]. They emerge "reborn", bound to the city and to the Coven.

It is both a ceremony and a legal act. A citizen who has not undergone the Tidal Lock is not a full member of the city and cannot hold a seat in a Lacus.

## Lead-Up
- **Education.** Citizens move through Novilunium (elementary), Corniculata (high school), and Plenilunium (university). Education is shaped around Coven life and prepares each citizen to choose a Lacus.
- **The Choice.** Each citizen picks one of the four Lacus: [[Lacus Somniorum]], [[Lacus Veris]], [[Lacus Timoris]], or [[Lacus Solitudinis]]. Family often pushes toward a parent's Lacus, as with [[Harald]]'s parents, but the choice is formally free. *(suggested: it cannot be changed afterward without Council approval.)*
- **The Moon Count.** Age is counted in true lunar months, so the date is set by the lunar cycle and not by the 28-day calendar month.

## The Rite *(suggested stages)*
1. **Vigil.** The candidate fasts and keeps watch through a night, supported by their cohort and family.
2. **Oath.** They name their Lacus and swear to protect Lux Lunae before witnesses from that Lacus.
3. **Descent.** They are fully immersed in a basin of [[Gleam]]. Attendants from [[Lacus Veris]] stand by.
4. **Emergence.** They are raised from the basin and recognised as reborn. The first breath is traditionally taken in front of the family.
5. **Welcome.** Their Lacus receives them with a ceremony of its own. Veris examines each of them afterward.

The ritual is done in cohorts and is public, with festivities that last through the evening.

## What People Notice Afterward
- **Most citizens feel nothing unusual** beyond a lasting calm, and the sense of belonging the ritual is meant to produce.
- **Astroderm.** A rare side effect (about 5% of the Coven according to the records of [[Lacus Solitudinis]]). The skin develops metallic markings. [[Harald]]'s dark purple skin has freckles that look like stars. Whether it is merely a rare consequence or something more is unknown.
- **Resilience.** Citizens vary in how much the rite affects them. Nobody understands why.
- **Ancestry.** Effects may vary by ancestry: Aetheris, Elves, Skykin, Humans, Faerie, and Fungrils. *(suggested: Fungrils and Faerie react differently. The Coven keeps records but not conclusions.)*

## Cohorts *(suggested)*
Citizens who undergo the rite in the same ceremony are called **lock-mates**. The bond is semi-official. Lock-mates may call on each other for favours across Lacus lines, and it is a social disgrace to betray one.

Cohort hook for the party: [[Harald]]'s own cohort, including any he shared with [[Visindi]] and [[Sunna]], might include people who know what he did before he left.

## Outsiders and Edge Cases
- **Captives and newcomers.** People brought to the city, such as the captured [[Ashen-Paw]] aboard the sky-barge, are a policy question. *(suggested: some are offered citizenship by way of the rite, which the Coven treats as a kindness and the captives experience as a sentence.)*
- **Those who refuse.** Citizens who won't go through with it lose their status and housing. They are not expelled, but their life becomes difficult.
- **Those who don't emerge.** *(suggested: a small fraction do not survive the Descent. The Coven records them as "returned to the moon" and does not publish the number.)*

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
- The Tidal Lock is a **mass seeding**. Every citizen is drowned in [[Gleam]] at twenty, which plants it in the body, where it expands slowly for the rest of their life.
- The Coven believes the rite **binds** citizens to the city. It is true in an unintended way: the Gleam inside them is connected to the Reagents, not to Lux Lunae.
- Most citizens are lucky or resilient and don't progress. **Why** most survive is unclear.
- **Astroderm** is an exception. The Gleam has gone into the skin instead of inward, which is why [[Harald]] kept his mind.
- The mind-controlling voice associated with the Coven may be easier to hear in the Descent. *(suggested: some candidates report a voice under the water that said their name.)*
- Every Lunarian who has done the rite is a potential Convergent. The Coven has built its entire society on that fact without knowing it.

## Hooks
- A cohort whose rite is delayed turns out to have been selected for Veris's study.
- A lock-mate shows up in a wellcity with a silvered fingertip and asks Harald for help.
- A captive from the Smoked Plains is scheduled for the rite on a date the PCs can reach.
- A citizen who heard the voice under the water has been quietly confined by [[Lacus Timoris]].
