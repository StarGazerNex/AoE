## Common Knowledge
Calium Arena is the biggest venue in [[Ardentia]]. It holds tens of thousands of people and hosts the city's largest concerts, fights and tournaments. [[Luster]]'s fans have packed it. To perform there is to have arrived.

## The Venue *(suggested)*
- **The Floor.** The main stage and standing area, where fans press against the barrier.
- **The Crown.** The top tier of private boxes, reserved for the families and operators who run the city.
- **Backstage.** A warren of studios, dressing rooms and holding corridors. Artists tend to stay on the stage side and never see the rest.
- **The Core.** A sealed machine floor beneath the stage that powers the lights, lifts and sound. Staff say it hums when the crowd is loudest.

## Luster at Calium
- Her shows have sold out the arena.
- She fell from the stage to the floor at one of them, and landed on her arm.
- After a show she goes straight to the bathroom to cool down with water on her wrists and neck.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(all suggested)*
- The Core is run by [[Gleam]], and it is the best-hidden Gleam installation in Ardentia.
- A full house raises its output. Crowds are not only the audience, they are the fuel.
- Luster's fall happened above the Core. Whether the stage lift failed on its own, or was made to fail, is not known.

## Hooks
- A stagehand tells a PC the lift under Luster's stage was changed the week before her fall.
- A tournament match is thrown, and the bookies at the Ring know the result before the fighters do.
- A fan collapses in the crowd and wakes up with a silvered fingertip.
