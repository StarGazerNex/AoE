## Common Knowledge
Ardentia is a city built around ambition, spectacle, risk, and the pursuit of power. People come hoping to become someone: to find an opportunity, close a lucrative deal, win fame in the arenas, or catch the attention of someone with influence. It is a dog-eat-dog city where fortune can change overnight and every glittering promise may conceal a trap.

It is famous for its massive arenas, which hold tens of thousands of spectators, and for its casinos, where visitors can lose everything in a single night. It offers every kind of entertainment, indulgence, and vice. To its supporters, it is the living heart of the continent, where talent, wealth, and ambition can make anyone a legend. To its critics, it is a glittering machine designed to consume the desperate and reward the ruthless.

Ardentia is a [[Wellcities|wellcity]], and one of the most important economic and cultural centers on the continent.

## History
**From fishing town to boom city.** Ardentia began as a small fishing town, unremarkable except for its location and its people. It became one of the first cities to discover [[Gleam]], and that changed everything. The substance enriched the surrounding soil, made the land unusually fertile, and drew the attention of powerful property owners in distant cities.

**The rush.** As word spread, investors, developers, and wealthy families arrived to claim land and exploit the new opportunities. That influx of money and ambition made the city grow at an extraordinary pace. Within a few years the former fishing town was a major wellcity.

**The rebuild.** The [[Gleam]]-driven boom reshaped the city physically as well as socially. New high-rise buildings and technology-filled districts sprawled inland and replaced the older parts of town.

## A Divided City
The city presents itself as a place of opportunity, but opportunity is not equally available. This divide is one of Ardentia's defining features.

| | **The new districts** | **The old town** |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Inland, sprawling | The original fishing town |
| **Who lives there** | The wealthy, the influential, and those who profit from entertainment, gambling, and speculation | Those who cannot afford the gleaming new districts |
| **Feel** | High-rises, advanced technology, luxury, spectacle | Abandoned, decaying |

Those who arrive seeking a better life often find themselves trapped in a cycle of debt, gambling, and exploitation.

## The Stage
Everything in Ardentia is designed to be seen, admired, envied, or consumed. People do not merely live there. They perform, compete, gamble, and struggle to rise above one another. The city rewards anyone who can capture attention, whether through talent, wealth, violence, beauty, or cunning.

- **Arenas.** The finest in the known world, with room for tens of thousands. [[Calium Arena]] is the biggest.
- **Casinos and gambling houses.** Everyone can play. Almost no one leaves ahead.
- **Performance halls and entertainment venues.** The home of the music industry, including [[Glow Gleam Records]].
- **Architecture.** Built to impress as much as to house people.

## Relationship With Deepsteel
[[Deepsteel]] was the birthplace of technology, but Ardentia understood how to use it best. The city took Deepsteel's innovations and applied them to entertainment, architecture, and spectacle. Deepsteel builds the machines and Ardentia puts them on show.

## Power and Governance *(suggested)*
Power sits with the families and investors who bought land early, the arena and casino operators, and the label and media houses. Formal government is real but works mostly as a referee between them. See [[Wellcities]] for the general model.

## Districts *(suggested)*
- **The Glass Mile.** The new inland core: high-rises, labels, penthouses.
- **The Ring.** The arena district around [[Calium Arena]], with its betting houses.
- **The Old Quay.** The original fishing town, now decaying, where the well first opened.
- **The Floor.** The casino quarter that never closes.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(all suggested)*
- Ardentia is a **nursery**, like every wellcity. The spectacle, the crowds, and the constant attention all feed the well.
- The boom is the surface expression of the well's clock. A great season at the arena may mean the well is advancing.
- The old town sits directly over the original well. The new districts are built on top of the debt of those who live there.

## Hooks
- A well opens in a part of the city that has never had one.
- A debt collector offers a PC a way out in exchange for a favor at [[Calium Arena]].
- A Deepsteel engineer is in town to quietly buy back an invention Ardentia has turned into a show.
- A rising star in the old town turns out to be Anointed and does not remember bathing.
