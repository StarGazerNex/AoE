## Common Knowledge
At the bottom of [[The Hollow]] lies a large lake of silver liquid. Deepsteel's faithful call it holy. It was dull and lifeless when Zaziel's followers arrived, and it came alive after his sacrifice. It is the end of the Descent, the site of the Giving, and the heart of Deepsteel's religion.

## Features *(suggested)*
- **Stations of the Descent.** The tiers along the crater wall mark the places where Zaziel's followers rested. The oldest districts at the rim are the first stations.
- **The Shore.** Shrines, hospices, and the rite of the Giving.
- **The Deep Gate.** Locked and quiet. Only the oldest preachers go beyond it.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
- It is a [[Gleam]] well, the largest the PCs will meet, and it has a clock.
- Each Giving adds to it.
- It is the likeliest source of engineered [[Convergence]] in [[Deepsteel]], since the technology and the lake are only a few tiers apart.

## Hooks
- The lake level rises a hand's width the night after a Giving.
- A guild engineer finds that the leyline-like patterns of the lake match [[Lux Lunae]]'s skystone foundations.
