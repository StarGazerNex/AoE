The Smoked Plains are a vast, wind-swept territory located far downriver from the skies where Lux Lunae now drifts. The region takes its name from the [[Ashen-Paw]] clan, the people who have lived there for generations. Before the civil war, the [[Ashen-Paw]] were renowned across the region as master artisans, storytellers, and deep-soil herbalists.

The [[Ashen-Paw]] were a tight-knit, nomadic community bound together by oral tradition. Their culture centered on communal hearth-gatherings, where elders passed down ancient history through music, story, and ritual. Craftsmen forged ironwork designed to endure for generations, while herbalists developed a deep understanding of the rivers, soil, and mountain weather. The [[Ashen-Paw]] lived in close rhythm with the land long before Gleam ever touched their soil.

Everything changed generations after Lux Lunae ascended into the heavens. A silver well ruptured in the heart of the Smoked Plains, releasing a strange and powerful substance into the region. Rather than rushing to build a city around it or bowing before it, the Ashen-Paw elders approached the well with caution and pragmatism.

They discovered that the mineral crust forming along the well’s edges could be dried and blended with mountain herbs. The result was a dense, silver-tinged leaf. When burned and inhaled through heavy iron pipes called [[Kiseru]], the smoke produced a very different effect from raw [[Gleam]]. It did not create mind-drowning euphoria or uncontrollable power. Instead, when taken in measured pulls, it granted calm, unyielding focus, strengthened the body, and sharpened tactical instincts.

In deeper, carefully controlled ritual doses, the smoke granted the elders vivid guiding visions. These visions helped them determine the best times to plant crops, resolve bitter disputes within the clan, and choose the proper offerings and seasonal festivities to honor the gods. For decades, this sacred smoke practice served as the moral and spiritual compass of the [[Ashen-Paw]] people.

The practice became known as the [[Smoke-Bound]] tradition. It was not simply a method of gaining power. It was a disciplined relationship with the land, the well, and the divine. The Ashen-Paw believed that strength came not from consuming Gleam directly, but from respecting its limits and using it with intention.

This balance was shattered when [[Lux Lunae]] learned of the clan’s practices. Seekers from [[Lacus Solitudinis]] and spymasters from [[Lacus Somniorum]] discovered the [[Ashen-Paw]]’s unique use of Gleam. The Coven saw the smoke discipline as both an asset to be seized and a threat to be erased. Rather than allowing another people to develop an independent relationship with Gleam, the Coven sought to monopolize it.

Hidden emissaries were sent into the Smoked Plains. They quietly supplied ambitious members of the [[Ashen-Paw]] with raw, unrefined Gleam, tempting them with the promise of direct power. They also introduced weapons forged from silver-infused steel, offering military advantage to those willing to abandon tradition.

The clan fractured into two hostile camps. The [[Smoke-Bound]] remained loyal to the old ways, using the kiseru and its controlled smoke rituals. The [[Gleam-drinkers]] embraced raw Gleam consumption and Coven-backed weaponry, believing that power should be taken directly rather than moderated through ritual.

What followed was a brutal and grinding civil war that lasted throughout Mog’el’s youth. The conflict was not simply a battle between two factions. It was a war between tradition and exploitation, discipline and greed, spiritual balance and unchecked power. The Coven did not need to conquer the [[Ashen-Paw]] directly. It only needed to divide them.

In the end, the [[Gleam-drinkers]] prevailed. Backed by Coven coin and superior weaponry, they slowly wore down the traditionalists. The final stand of the [[Smoke-Bound]] was not a grand victory but a desperate retreat into the hills. The [[Ashen-Paw]] were scattered, captured, or killed. Their sacred practices were driven underground, and their homeland fell under the influence of the very forces that had torn it apart.

The Smoked Plains remain a scarred region, marked by the legacy of the silver well, the civil war, and Coven interference. It is a place where the land itself remembers what happened, and where the [[Ashen-Paw]]’s surviving traditions may still hold the key to resisting Lux Lunae’s influence.
