## Common Knowledge
Nightvale is a small forest town at the western edge of the [[The Evenwood|Evenwood]], known for carpentry and for the fireflies that give it its name. It values craft, community and building a life with one's hands. [[Titania]]'s family lives here. The horseless carriage line from [[Deepsteel]] ends here.

## Places *(suggested)*
- **The Joiners' Row.** Workshops, including [[Viryx]]'s and [[Wellia]]'s.
- **The Open Door.** [[Dwight]]'s clinic.
- **Nora's Kitchen.** The inn that feeds the town.
- **The Roof.** The family's roof where Allium watched the forest.
- **The Basket Stone.** A mossy stone at the forest edge, where the twins were found.
- **The Silver Seep.** A new well in a clearing a mile into the forest. Opens in Act I.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Nightvale is on the edge of what the [[The Vael|Vael]] would call the first harvest. It is small, kind and close to [[Evenfall]]. A seed here costs the Reagents almost nothing.

## Hooks
- The Seep opens on the night of the crash.
- Three delegations arrive at once.
- A family argument over whether to trust Evenfall.
