Lux Lunae is a floating city that was once part of the older city of [[The Hollow|Tellus]]. During an event known as the Ascendancy, the city altered the landscape and took flight in an unprecedented magical feat. This event separated [[Lux Lunae]] from [[The Hollow]], the portion of the old city that did not rise into the sky.

The Ascendancy occurred shortly after Gleam first appeared. Because Lux Lunae was able to understand and harness arcane power from Gleam so soon after its emergence, the wider world came to view the city and the Lunarian Coven as diviners, seers, and foreseers. To outsiders, Lux Lunae appears to possess an almost prophetic understanding of the future. In reality, the city’s power and influence are built upon knowledge that its rulers carefully control.

Gleam is central to Lux Lunae’s identity. It is the arcane substance or force that enabled the city’s Ascendancy, sustains its floating foundations, and plays a role in the Tidal Lock ritual. The Lunarian Coven treats Gleam-related knowledge as highly sensitive, preserving it within the city and using it to maintain both its physical survival and political influence.

The Lunarian Coven is the governing and magical institution of Lux Lunae. It is intensely secretive, viewing information as a means of manipulation and influence. Through control of knowledge, the Coven is able to shape world politics from the shadows while preserving the safety, autonomy, and prestige of the floating city.

The Coven is divided into four factions, known as Lacus. Each faction has a distinct responsibility in maintaining and governing the city.

[[Lacus Somniorum]] is the faction of visionaries, seers, oracle-witches, strategists, and spiritual guides. Its members study the alignment of the stars and the phases of the moon in order to predict atmospheric changes, planar tears, and future threats to the floating island. They also use dream-walking and scrying to investigate mysteries, resolve high-level disputes, and shape the long-term policies of Lux Lunae.

[[Lacus Veris]] is the faction of healers, alchemists, and elemental and growth-focused witches. Its members maintain the city’s suspended gardens, crop terraces, and clean rainfall. They also oversee sanctuaries, public health, alchemy, and healing. In a city suspended high above the ground, Veris ensures that the population can survive, eat, recover from injury, and remain healthy.

[[Lacus Timoris]] is the faction of wardens, combat witches, and shadow-weavers. It maintains the magical wards, cloaking illusions, and air-defense barriers that protect Lux Lunae from external threats such as dragon raids, sky pirates, and rival nations. Timoris also enforces order within the city, rooting out corruption and dangerous magic before it can destabilize the island.

[[Lacus Solitudinis]] is the faction of scholars, archivists, arcana-mechanics, and quiet keepers of deep secrets. Its members gather, transcribe, and preserve ancient sky-tomes, celestial maps, and forbidden arcane artifacts. Most importantly, Solitudinis maintains the Gleam technology that keeps Lux Lunae airborne, including the ancient leyline engines and skystone foundations that prevent the city from falling.

Above these four factions stands the [[Lunarian Coven]] Council. The Council contains five seats. Four belong to a witch from each of the Lacus factions. The fifth belongs to the Moonarch Superior, the mysterious ruler of Lux Lunae.

No one knows the identity of the [[Moonarch Superior]]. In every public appearance, the Moonarch is shrouded in a black veil that prevents anyone from perceiving their facial features. This concealment has become one of the city’s most enduring mysteries. It is unclear whether the Moonarch hides their identity for protection, tradition, political power, or something far more sinister.

Lux Lunae is populated by six main ancestries: Aetheris, Elves, Skykin, Humans, Faerie, and Fungrils. Despite their differences, all citizens are bound together by the city’s shared dependence on the Coven, Gleam, and the floating island itself.

The city also has a three-tiered education system. Novilunium serves as elementary education, Corniculata as high school, and Plenilunium as university. Education is closely tied to Coven life, since citizens must eventually choose a Lacus and take on responsibility for the city’s survival.

At the age of twenty, or two hundred and forty-eight moons by Lunarian reckoning, every citizen must choose a Lacus. They swear an oath to protect the floating city and undergo the Tidal Lock. During this rite of passage, they are drowned in Gleam and emerge reborn. The ritual is both a coming-of-age ceremony and a binding act of loyalty to Lux Lunae.

Despite its beauty and apparent stability, Lux Lunae is built upon unanswered questions. No one fully understands what caused the Ascendancy, why only part of Tellus rose into the sky, or what became of The Hollow. No one knows the true origin of Gleam, the identity of the Moonarch Superior, or the nature of the mind-controlling voice associated with the Coven. These mysteries form the foundation of the city’s power—and potentially its greatest danger.
