Deepsteel is a city built inside [[The Hollow]], the crater left by the Ascendancy. It is the birthplace of technology. While [[Lux Lunae]] kept the knowledge of [[Gleam]] and became a city of seers and secrets, Deepsteel answered the same loss with iron, steam, and wire.

**Why it exists.** Tellus's survivors found themselves at the bottom of a hole with walls of raw ore and no magic of their own to compete with the sky. Deepsteel's founding principle is that nobody should ever again be left behind by something they can't understand. Its culture prizes open schematics, guilds, and apprenticeship, the opposite of the Coven's hoarded secrets.

**Layout.** The city descends in tiers along the crater walls, with foundries and conduits at depth and the oldest districts at the rim. Cables, pipes, and lifts connect every level, which is the literal "wires" of the campaign title.

**Relationship to Gleam.** Deepsteel officially distrusts it, but its engineers know [[Gleam]]-forged metal holds an edge forever, and the temptation to combine the two is the city's quiet crisis. This is the likeliest origin of engineered [[Convergence]].

**Connections**
- [[Ardentia]] takes Deepsteel's inventions and turns them into spectacle and profit.
- Horseless carriages have reached as far as [[Nightvale]].

**Open questions**
- Who runs it: a guild council, a single magnate, or someone who isn't what they seem?
- Does Deepsteel hold the Coven responsible for the Hollow, and do its people know where the missing half went?
