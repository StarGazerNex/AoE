The Hollow is the vast crater left behind when [[Lux Lunae]] tore free of the old city of Tellus during the Ascendancy. Once the place where Lux Lunae stood, before it lifted into the air, it is now the site of [[Deepsteel]]. Tellus was the name of the whole city. "The Hollow" is what its people started calling the missing piece: the part of the ground that went up with the sky city. The walls of the crater expose deep strata of ore and metal that no one had ever been able to reach, and that exposure made [[Deepsteel]] possible. Those who stayed behind watched part of their city, and their neighbours, rise into the sky and never come back.
