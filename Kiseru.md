A kiseru is a long, heavy iron pipe used by the [[Smoke-Bound]] to smoke the silver-tinged leaf made from [[Gleam]] crust and mountain herbs. [[Mog'el]] carries his father's kiseru, which doubles as a weapon. Each [[Ashen-Paw]] kiseru is made to last generations, so the pipe holds the clan's history as well as its ritual. Each kiseru's bowl is engraved with the names of those who carried it before.
