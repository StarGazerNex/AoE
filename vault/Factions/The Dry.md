Communities that refuse the [[Gleam]]. See [[Overview]] and [[Distinctions]]: they are not simply the good guys.

## Camps *(suggested)*
- **The Salt Compact.** Organised, based at [[Saltreach]]. Hierarch [[Odrin Haske]]; pragmatist [[Mara Quillon]].
- **The Ash Hand.** Militants who burn supply lines.
- **The Walkers.** Families who keep moving away from wells.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Some are right to be afraid. The Salt Compact's seals slow wells but never kill them. Haske wants to blow the lake; Quillon knows that would make it worse.
