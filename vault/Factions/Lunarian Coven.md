The governing and magical institution of [[Lux Lunae]]. Secretive. It treats information as a means of influence. See [[Lux Lunae]] for the full description.

## Structure
- **Four Lacus:** [[Lacus Somniorum]], [[Lacus Veris]], [[Lacus Timoris]], [[Lacus Solitudinis]].
- **The Council:** five seats. One witch per Lacus, and the [[Moonarch Superior]].
- **Entry:** the [[Tidal Lock]] at age twenty.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
- The Coven is run by a Reagent through the Moonarch. Most members, including the Council, do not know.
- Its dissidents are in the [[Quiet Lamp]], inside Solitudinis.
- Its secret police, [[Lacus Timoris]], would hunt Harald.
