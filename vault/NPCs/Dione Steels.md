**Role:** singer. The voice of *Where's My Love*. One of the earliest signings of [[Glow Gleam Records]].

## Common Knowledge
Beloved, reclusive, ageless-looking. [[Madeline Lomazin]] wrote her biggest songs. [[Luster]]'s favourite.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Dione is Anointed, the oldest example the label has, and has not slept in years. *Where's My Love* is a lament for [[Merrow Vale]], her partner, who left. Dione has been told not to leave the label and has stayed, because the Sweets keep her alive. She is the first person to tell Luster not to take one.

## Hooks
- She sings with Luster in the Closing, if she has been won over.
- She gives Luster Merrow's address in [[Brinewake]].
