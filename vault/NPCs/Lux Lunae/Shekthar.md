**Role:** Harald's father. 50. [[Lacus Somniorum]]. Taught him dream magic in secret. See [[Factions/Lacus Somniorum|Lacus Somniorum]].

## Personality
Patient, gentle, mildly absent-minded.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Shekthar suspects Somniorum's visions are borrowed. He taught his son dream magic because he hoped one of them would be able to look *under* the foresight. He cannot act openly. In Act III he walks the dream with Harald into the Voice.

## Hooks
- He sends a coded dream.
- Somniorum interrogates him about his son.
