**Role:** Harald's younger sister. 19. Skykin. See [[Harald]].

## Personality
Quick, protective, tired of being "Harald's sister". She idolises him and is angry that he left.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Her Tidal Lock is in a few months, scheduled for the day [[Lux Lunae]] arrives over [[The Hollow]]. She has not chosen a Lacus. [[Lacus Veris]] has been courting her, because they have noticed she resists Gleam unusually well.

## Hooks
- She sends a message by Quiet Lamp courier: *I'm choosing in the autumn. Come and talk me out of it, or into it.*
- In Act III she is in the cohort at the Great Giving.
