**Role:** Plenilunium teacher who left [[Lucens Liquidus]] on [[Harald]]'s desk. Member of the [[Quiet Lamp]].
**Personality:** dry, precise, kind to students, afraid of nothing except being wrong.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
Oriel chose Harald because of his Astroderm and Shekthar's dream-magic. He is the Lamp's contact in Act II and keeps the original Ledger of Wells. He is arrested at the end of Act I and rescued in Act II.
