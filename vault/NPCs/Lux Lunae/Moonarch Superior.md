**Role:** ruler of [[Lux Lunae]]. Fifth seat of the [[Lunarian Coven]] Council. Veiled in every public appearance.

## Common Knowledge
Nobody has ever seen their face. The veil is black and full.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
- The Moonarch is **Sael Moranthe**, the first Moonarch and the archmage who led the Ascendancy. She wrote [[Lucens Liquidus]] while still herself, then Converged. She has worn the veil since the Ascendancy and was taken by a Reagent about a century ago. A Reagent wears her body now and does not know about the book.
- The Moonarch is **the Hand**. The Reagent in her is the one coordinating the Chorus.
- Under the veil: silver, still, and, for one second at the end of Act III, her own face.

## Hooks
- The vision in the tome: the Moonarch's hands over thousands of drooling citizens.
- The Moonarch asks for a private audience with [[Harald]].
