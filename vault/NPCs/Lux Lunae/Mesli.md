**Role:** Harald's mother. 45. [[Lacus Timoris]], a combat witch and shadow-weaver. See [[Factions/Lacus Timoris|Lacus Timoris]].

## Personality
Watchful, dry, loves by planning for the worst.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
She is the one Timoris would send, and she has been told to. She cannot refuse openly. She intends to find him first, and decide there. She knows the wards keep citizens in, and that the Moonarch controls them.

## Hooks
- Act I: she arrives to warn, not arrest.
- Act II: she hunts him, then asks to be taken with him.
- Act III: she opens the wards at the Foundations.
