**Role:** Aetheris warrior-priest of [[Hesperion]]'s Sanctuaryum and [[Nymestria]]'s mentor since childhood.
**Appearance:** tall, white-and-gold armour, wings of silver-edged feathers.

## Common Knowledge
Hesperion's most honoured teacher. He trained the next Sacrifice from childhood, and the city treats him as a father to the orphans.

## Personality
Warm, exacting, loyal to the god. Praises quietly. When angry he becomes very still.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Ambriel loves Nymestria and believes the Sacrifice is the greatest gift a child can be given. He is Anointed and half-aware that the god's voice is not wholly kind. He has never let himself ask. When she fled he was not only furious but afraid for her. He will hunt her and, when cornered, can be turned: he only needs one honest answer from her.

## Hooks
- He offers to let her come home if she kneels.
- He appears over the battle at [[Crystalight]], and again in Act III with the Sanctuaryum.

Answers [[Nymestria]]'s question "who leads it above Ambriel?": [[Thessaly Orrin]].
