**Role:** Harald's childhood friend. 23. Arcane prodigy. Chose [[Lacus Somniorum]]. See [[Harald]].

## Personality
Serene, practical, the tall one who defended him. Blunt about her feelings and about his fighting.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Sunna has heard a voice under the water since her Tidal Lock and has told nobody. It speaks in her visions. She believes it is spirits. Some of the foresight of Somniorum is the voice; she has learned how to refuse it, and that is why she is the only seer in Somniorum who is not taken. In Act I she is sent with [[Visindi]] to bring Harald home. She stays inside afterwards.

## Hooks
- A vision shows her Harald with his hands out in a lake.
- In Act III she is the channel the Moonarch uses to speak to the party. She is still in there.
