**Role:** Deepsteel biologist. Head of Project Subject:555, author of the logs in [[CIX]]'s note.

## Common Knowledge
Respected in the guilds as a quiet genius. Few know what the project was.

## Personality
Gentle, sleepless, funny in short bursts. Sings to herself in the lab on calm days.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
- Her lab, the **Cradle Level**, was funded by the [[The Silent Seat|Silent Seat]]. She believed it was a medical project. She did not know whose DNA the sample was until after she had named him.
- She designed the Gleam core in [[Luster]]'s arm. Roger commissioned it. She knows what she put in it.
- She raised CIX as a son and also as a subject, and cannot forgive herself either way.
- She sent CIX away to protect him. In Act II she is taken or killed.

## Hooks
- Her last log: *he was never meant to say no. I hope he does.*
