**Role:** the Sacrifice before [[Nymestria]]. Older girl at the orphanage.

## Common Knowledge
Celebrated and veiled; the Sanctuaryum says she "ascended".

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Dahlia was not killed. The ritual puts the Sacrifice into a chrysalis beneath the well, preserved while a Reagent seeds her. She is the **Mother of the Well** at [[Hesperion]]. The "invader" who helped break Nymestria's chains was Dahlia, reaching out through the Gleam. She is still partly herself and she is in pain. Each earlier Sacrifice is there too; Nymestria's visions of previous Sacrifices were their voices.

## Hooks
- Nymestria dreams of a door and a girl humming a lullaby.
- She wants to be *cut free*. She cannot ask.
- In Act III she is carried to the Giving in a chrysalis.
