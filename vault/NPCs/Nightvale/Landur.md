**Role:** human twin, sun-kissed, black hair with a blue hue. Town jokester. See [[Titania]].

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Landur never apologised and never forgave. He is closer to the truth than anyone in Nightvale: the twins were left at the forest edge by [[Ysolde Marrow]] of [[Saltreach]], a Dry mother who knew what the forest would do to a child. They resist Gleam better than anyone.
