**Role:** [[Titania]]'s biological brother. Elf ranger, former protector of [[Nightvale]]. See [[Titania]].

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
- About a year before the campaign, Allium followed a silver light into the forest and did not come back. He drank from a spring that was the first seep.
- He is now a Convergent **Warden of the Boughs** in [[Evenfall]]. He still climbs roofs at night to watch the forest.
- **He is not fully gone.** The Reagent in him has copied him so well that something of him is held in the copy. That is why he can answer questions only he could answer.
- In Act II Tia finds him. In Act III he stands at the Deep Gate, then as the Watch.

## Hooks
- A Convergent Warden says something only Allium would know.
- He tells Tia to stop looking. His voice is kind.
