**Role:** faun, healthcare lead of [[Nightvale]]. Closest to [[Titania]] in age. See [[Titania]].

## Personality
Large, warm, never refuses anyone.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
His clinic, **the Open Door**, has seen the first signs of Gleam in the town. He is the first to notice a patient with a silver fingertip and says nothing in order to protect them.

## Hooks
- He asks the party to help treat the wounded.
- He is the one who tells Tia the truth about the twins.
