**Role:** Harald's friend and "twin". 22. Chose [[Lacus Timoris]]. See [[Harald]].

## Personality
Impulsive, loud, the troublemaker who took the rigidity out of Harald's life.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Visindi was assigned to Timoris tracking because he knew Harald best. He has never broken an oath, but he does not like this one. He defects at the end of Act I or the start of Act II, once he sees what Timoris does to citizens who hear the voice. He is the party's best skiff pilot, and a liability on a stealth mission.

## Hooks
- He steals a skiff to follow the party.
- He asks [[Mesli]] a question that cannot be answered.
