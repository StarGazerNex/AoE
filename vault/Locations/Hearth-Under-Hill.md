## Common Knowledge
A hidden camp in the hills at the edge of the [[Smoked Plains]], where surviving [[Smoke-Bound]] keep the rite. [[Grandmother Vess]], [[Tobrek and Oma]] and others live here.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
The hearth-stone here is carved with the names of every kiseru-bearer. [[Mog'el]]'s name is the last.
