## Common Knowledge
A rebel enclave on the edge of [[Hesperion]]. Smuggled and scarce resources become radios, vehicles, small devices and weapons. [[Nymestria]] spent two months here and was made over.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Crystalight fell after it was exposed. A few survivors are in the dark corridors. [[Tamsin Ashgrove]] is in the Sanctuary's cells.
