## Common Knowledge
The capital of the [[The Dry|Dry]]. A city of white stone on the northern salt flats, built to keep wells sealed. [[Mara Quillon]] and [[Odrin Haske]] lead rival camps.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Saltreach is not safe. The salt seals slow wells but never kill them. Many of the city's own children carry traces of Gleam. [[Ysolde Marrow]] lives here.
