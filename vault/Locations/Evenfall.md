## Common Knowledge
A wellcity hidden in the [[The Evenwood|Evenwood]], built in the hollows of giant trees around a Heartwell. Quiet, green, beautiful. Governed by Wardens of the Boughs; many are Convergent. Furniture from [[Nightvale]] decorates its halls.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
Evenfall is the nearest nursery. It collects the town's runaways, rangers and lost travellers. [[Allium]] is a Warden. [[Mother Linden]] leads.

## Hooks
- A welcome feast that never ends.
- A path of lanterns that seems to know your name.
