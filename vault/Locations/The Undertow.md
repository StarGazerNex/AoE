## Common Knowledge
The route Nymestria took out of [[Hesperion]]: dark corridors, an underground lake swum underwater, the underworld of giant spiders and larvae and walls of screaming dead, and a hydra at the exit above the Gull's Throat.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
The Undertow was dug by the first Sacrifices' families. Its walls remember them. It ends where the sea-cliffs meet [[Brinewake]]'s waters.
