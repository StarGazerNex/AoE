## Common Knowledge
A cavern-city beneath the western sea-cliffs. A theocratic wellcity lit by Gleam. Home of the Sanctuaryum, the orphanage-school, the golden bathing rooms, the statue of the "savior", and the Pale Wardens. The surface is "Upstairs". [[Nymestria]]'s home.

## Districts *(suggested)*
- **The Orphanage and Training Ground.** Where [[Ambriel]] taught.
- **The Gilded Quarter.** Feasts, balls, the Sacrifice's gold bathing room.
- **The Well Hall.** Altar in the middle, chanting around it.
- **The Outskirts.** Where [[Crystalight]] hides.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
The well under the altar is a nursery of its own. Each Sacrifice is chrysalised beneath it; [[Dahlia]] is there. Archon [[Thessaly Orrin]] speaks for the god.

## Proposed answers to Nymestria's open questions
- The sanctum is **Hesperion**; the governing body is the Sanctuaryum led by Orrin.
- "Upstairs" is the surface. The underground route is [[The Undertow]].
- The ritual is a Gleam-fed seeding; see [[The Vael]].
