## Common Knowledge
A Tidekin city at the foot of the Gull's Throat cliff, partly submerged. Neutral traders. Their Tide-song is the oldest known way to quiet a well. [[Pell]] is from here. [[Merrow Vale]] hid here.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only *(suggested)*
Brinewake has a drowned well it has kept calm for generations, by singing. This is the answer to "why do Tidekin not Converge": they never stopped singing.
