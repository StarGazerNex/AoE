Districts of [[Lux Lunae]] *(all suggested)*.
- **[[Plenilunium]].** The university. [[Harald]]'s desk, [[Oriel Vantasse]]'s classroom.
- **The Veil Hall.** The Moonarch's chamber, where the Council sits.
- **The Skystone Foundations.** The engines and skystone that keep the city airborne, tended by [[Lacus Solitudinis]]. The place to slow its fall.
- **The Undersky.** Docks for sky-barges and skiffs.
- **The Basin.** Where the [[Tidal Lock]] is held.
