**Goal:** Gather the six Keys, each held by a different place, while the world moves toward High Water.
**Great Tide:** Rising to **High Water**.
**Suggested levels:** 4-8.

## Structure: Five Roads
Play in any order. Each road gives a Key for [[The Closing]] and ticks the Clock if ignored.

| Road | Led by | Where | Key | Key NPCs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Watcher's Road** | [[Titania]] | [[Evenfall]] | *The Wake*: how to call a Convergent back by name | [[Allium]], Mother Linden |
| **The Hollow Road** | [[Nymestria]] | [[Hesperion]], [[Crystalight]], [[The Undertow]] | *The Severance*: cut a seed without killing the host | [[Dahlia]], [[Ambriel]], [[Old Morel]] |
| **The Hearth Road** | [[Mog'el]] | [[Smoked Plains]], [[Hearth-Under-Hill]] | *The Hearth*: the measured rite | [[Grandmother Vess]], [[Tobrek and Oma]], [[Hollin Dray]] |
| **The Sky Road** | [[Harald]] | [[Lux Lunae]], [[Plenilunium]] | *The Lantern*: the Ledger of Wells and the Astroderm secret | [[Oriel Vantasse]], [[Shekthar]], [[Kaylie]] |
| **The Silver Road** | [[Luster]] and [[CIX]] | [[Deepsteel]], [[The Silver Lake]], [[Ardentia]], [[Brinewake]] | *The Voice* (Tide-song) and *The Anchor* (the Deep Gate) | [[Jophie]], [[Dione Steels]], [[Merrow Vale]], [[Pell]] |

## Recommended Order
1. **The Silver Road** first if the party followed Luster and CIX to Nightvale.
2. **The Hearth Road** next, to give Mog'el room for grief.
3. **The Watcher's Road** and **The Hollow Road** in the middle.
4. **The Sky Road** last. It is the most dangerous and the most personal for Harald.

## Complications
- **[[Visindi]] and [[Sunna]]** follow the party. Visindi defects when he sees the Tidal Lock records. Sunna stays inside and becomes a spy, then a channel.
- **[[Mesli]]** hunts Harald in earnest. Her conversation with him is the Act II centrepiece.
- **[[Roger Lomazin]]** offers Luster everything to come home.
- **[[Ambriel]]** takes the field against Nymestria.
- **The Chorus** begins. Convergent in different cities repeat the same sentence. This is the world's sign that the Reagents are coordinating.

## Midpoint Reversal
After the third road: the [[Moonarch Superior]] speaks to the entire continent through the Convergent. The message is calm. It promises the Great Giving, offers every citizen a place at the lake, and invites the world to come home. The wellcities cheer.
**Reveal 6:** The wells have been counting. Folio II names the date.

## Low Point: High Water
- [[Lux Lunae]] begins its Return and turns toward [[The Hollow]].
- Allium arrives in Nightvale. The Seep wakes and takes someone the party loves (default: [[Figment]], who is not seen again until Act III).
- [[Jophie]] is taken or dies protecting the lab.
- [[Kaylie]]'s Tidal Lock is scheduled for the day the sky city arrives.
- The Coven recalls every citizen to the city. [[Sunna]] is silent.

## Reveals
6. The wells are counting toward a date.
7. The lake is the original well.
8. The Ascendancy was a seeding. Lux Lunae is a brood-ship.
9. [[CIX]] was built to be a Hatchling's vessel.
10. The Moonarch is the first Moonarch, still alive and no longer themselves.
11. Convergent can be woken, but not by force.
