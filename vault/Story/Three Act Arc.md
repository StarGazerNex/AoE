## The One-Paragraph Version
A silver well opens at the edge of a quiet town the same night a stolen sky-barge crashes into its forest and a wounded assassin falls out of the sky. Six people with six very different wounds are thrown together. They learn that [[Gleam]] is a seed, that [[Lux Lunae]]'s foresight is borrowed, that the Coven's rite is a mass seeding, and that something from beyond the world, the [[The Vael|Vael]], is bringing every well to its last mark at once. The only thing that can stop it was hidden in a century-old book and needs exactly the six of them, each giving what they are best at, and each paying for it.

## How to Run It
- **Everything in Act II is order-flexible.** Five roads, any order, each with a Key. The Clock decides pressure, not a script.
- **Every PC gets a road and a Closing role.** If a player leaves, the role passes to an NPC and the story changes tone but not shape.
- **Secrets are staged.** What the table learns is in [[Act I - Surface]], [[Act II - Undertow]] and [[Act III - Return]] as numbered Reveals.
