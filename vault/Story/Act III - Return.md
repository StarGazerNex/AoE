**Goal:** Reach [[The Silver Lake]], hold the six roles, and perform [[The Closing]] before the Eclosion.
**Great Tide:** **High Water** to **Slack Water** to **Eclosion**.
**Suggested levels:** 9-12.

## Chapter 1: The Gathering at Rimhall
The party needs allies. [[Rimhall]] is the only place big enough to hold them.
- **[[Deepsteel]]'s guild council** (leader [[Ilsabet Kroth]]) votes on whether to defend the lake or evacuate. The Silent Seat is revealed.
- **The Dry** ([[Mara Quillon]], [[Odrin Haske]]) bring salt and explosives. Haske wants to blow the lake. Quillon knows that only makes it worse.
- **The Smoke-Bound survivors** bring the hearth.
- **The Tidekin** ([[Pell]]) bring the tide-song.
- **Lux Lunae dissidents**: [[Oriel Vantasse]] and the [[Quiet Lamp]], [[Shekthar]], [[Mesli]], if won over.
- **Reveal 12:** The Closing needs six roles. Folio III names them. Each PC realises which role is theirs.

## Chapter 2: The Descent
[[Lux Lunae]] comes down over [[The Hollow]]. Choose how the party meets it:
- **The Foundations:** Harald and Mesli board the sky city and reach the [[Lux Lunae Districts|Skystone Foundations]] to slow its fall.
- **The Veil Hall:** Harald, Visindi and Sunna confront the Moonarch. The veil lifts.
- **The Rim:** the others hold [[Rimhall]] against the Chorus.

## Chapter 3: The Great Giving
The faithful of Deepsteel, the cohort of Lux Lunae and the wellcity pilgrims walk down to the lake. [[Kaylie]]'s cohort is among them.
- **[[Ambriel]]** arrives with the Sanctuaryum. [[Dahlia]] is carried in a chrysalis.
- **[[Allium]]** stands at the Deep Gate.
- **[[Roger Lomazin]]** is in the crowd. [[Dione Steels]] is at the front, singing.

## Chapter 4: The Closing
Run as a ritual encounter. See [[The Closing]].
1. Each PC names what they are doing it for.
2. The roles begin in any order. Each has a scene and a roll.
3. The Hatchling rises. It uses the Voice of the lake, which sounds like someone each PC lost.
4. [[CIX]] stands in the lake and says no.
5. The prices are paid.
