Each arc starts from what the player's own note says is the **wound** and **gift**, and ends in a Closing role.

## Harald: The Lantern
- **Gift:** curiosity, scholarship, dream-magic from [[Shekthar]], the courage to ask the question.
- **Wound:** loyalty to a city that may be a lie; the frail boy [[Sunna]] defended; the fear that the [[Tidal Lock]] made him part of it.
- **Act I:** reads the first folio. Visindi and Sunna show up. He does not go home.
- **Act II:** the Sky Road. Mesli. Kaylie's countdown. He learns his Astroderm is the Gleam held at the surface.
- **Act III:** he stands at the Foundations and in the Veil Hall, then in the Closing. He takes the flood into his skin.
- **Ending question:** does he go home, and to what?

## Mog'el: The Hearth
- **Gift:** discipline, endurance, a war-survivor's judgment, the Smoke-Bound rite.
- **Wound:** he held the barricade and lost; he survived; his clan is scattered.
- **Act I:** frees the captives. Learns he is not the last of his people.
- **Act II:** the Hearth Road. He finds [[Tobrek and Oma]] and [[Grandmother Vess]], and faces [[Hollin Dray]].
- **Act III:** becomes the elder the rite needs. Smokes the last shared rite.
- **Ending question:** who carries the pipe after him?

## Titania: The Watch
- **Gift:** the ranger's patience, [[Pipin]], humour, the instinct to guard.
- **Wound:** [[Allium]]'s disappearance and what she said to the twins that night. See [[Figment]] and [[Landur]].
- **Act I:** hears the forest hum. Recognises something only Allium would know.
- **Act II:** the Watcher's Road. Finds Allium. Learns the Wake. Reconciles with the twins.
- **Act III:** holds the circle and calls the taken by name, Allium first.
- **Ending question:** can she let him go?

## Luster: The Voice
- **Gift:** a voice, charm, the trust of tens of thousands, a mother who writes without Gleam.
- **Wound:** the shadow of her parents' influence; an arm she did not choose; a career built on something she did not know.
- **Act I:** the lift, the Sweet, the demo, the Seep. She finds her voice does something.
- **Act II:** the Silver Road. Leaves the label. Learns Tide-song from [[Pell]] and [[Merrow Vale]]. Faces [[Roger Lomazin]].
- **Act III:** sings the Closing. The arm is the price she chooses.
- **Ending question:** does she sing again, and for whom?

## Nymestria: The Edge
- **Gift:** lethal skill, stealth, poison, a lifetime of watching how people die.
- **Wound:** she was made into a weapon and a sacrifice, and she killed.
- **Act I:** hides her past; the party learns it anyway.
- **Act II:** the Hollow Road. Returns for [[Dahlia]]. Faces [[Ambriel]]. Learns the Severance: cut without killing.
- **Act III:** severs the seeds from the Chorus and from herself.
- **Ending question:** what is she once she is not a weapon?

## CIX: The Anchor
- **Gift:** engineered strength, loyalty, a guardian's patience, hearing nobody else has.
- **Wound:** he was made, not born, to a purpose he did not choose. His maternal figure ([[Jophie]]) was also his handler.
- **Act I:** meets Luster, whose voice calms him as Jophie's did.
- **Act II:** the Silver Road. Learns he is a vessel and what for. Finds Jophie's last log.
- **Act III:** stands in the lake and says no.
- **Ending question:** what is a guardian when there is no one left to guard?

## Cross-Links
- Luster and CIX: her voice calms him, and he guards her from the label.
- Harald and Mog'el: the Gleam held at the surface and the smoke taken in measure are two halves of the same answer.
- Tia and Nymestria: both lost someone to a bigger power. One wants to wake him, the other has learned that waking means cutting.
- Mog'el and Nymestria: both were children of a faith turned against them.
