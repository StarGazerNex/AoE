The ending depends on how many roles hold and what each player pays. See [[The Closing]].

## The Seal
All six roles hold fully. The wells go still, the Gleam is dormant, and the world loses what it gained: the fast healing, the early crops, the edge that never dulls.
- [[Lux Lunae]] must land. [[Deepsteel]] helps it. A new city is built in [[The Hollow]]. The Coven and Deepsteel share what they know.
- Convergent hosts wake, if the Watch and Edge held.
- The [[Wellcities]] shrink. The Dry are not vindicated, only relieved.

## The Tide Turned
Five roles hold and the Hearth is kept. The Vael are cut away, but Gleam stays as a plain, dangerous resource, handled by the [[Smoke-Bound]] method.
- A new order of Hearth-keepers forms. Mog'el is its first elder.
- Wells quiet but need tending forever.

## The Flawed Eclosion
Three or fewer roles hold. The Hatchling rises but without a mind of its own. It is neither the Vael nor the world's. It is the lake's.
- This is the dark ending. Play it as a new campaign.

## Epilogues by Character
Pick one for each PC from their note. A few suggested:
- **[[Harald]]:** returns to Plenilunium, teaching what he knows.
- **[[Mog'el]]:** founds a hearth in the Hollow.
- **[[Titania]]:** is back on the roof of the family home, watching an ordinary forest.
- **[[Luster]]:** records an album with no label.
- **[[Nymestria]]:** learns to fly again, slowly.
- **[[CIX]]:** an open door. Either the lake, or the road.
