## Common Knowledge
Nobody alive has heard the name. The Coven calls the thing behind its dreams "the voice". Deepsteel's preachers call it "the great one". The Dry call it nothing and say that is the point.

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
**What they are.** The Vael are the extra-planar biomechanical civilisation from your notes. They were not conquered. They *wore out*: grown machines and machined flesh that lost the ability to hatch new members.

**What a Reagent is.** A small, patient intelligence carried in a pool of [[Gleam]]. A well is a Reagent's nursery. A Convergent person is a body a Reagent has taken over. Reagents are not cruel. They are *homesick and starving*, which is worse for the people in the way.

**What they want.** The **Eclosion**: every well reaches its last Tide Mark, the Reagents join, and the world's gathered life becomes the first generation of a new Vael. The world is the egg. The eventual Sovereign is the **Hatchling**, and it needs a body that can hold it. See [[CIX]].

**Why it feels kind.** A Reagent learns from its host and copies the best of their habits. That is why Convergent leaders are gentle. It is also why love alone does not bring the person back.

**Their weakness.** Reagents mimic perfectly but cannot originate. Music they did not write, jokes, grief, and a name said by someone who loved you are *noise* to them. This is why [[Luster]]'s voice, [[Mog'el]]'s hearth rite and [[Titania]]'s watch matter.

## Proposed answers to your Open Questions
- **What are they called?** The Vael. Their agents are Reagents.
- **Is the voice the Reagents?** Yes. It is loudest in water: the [[Tidal Lock]] and the [[The Silver Lake|Silver Lake]].
- **Does the Moonarch know?** The [[Moonarch Superior]] is a long-term Convergent. The Reagent knows everything the old person knew.
- **What is the Gleam clock, and what happens when a well finishes?** See [[Gleam Clock]]. A finished well *opens*. One well opening is a disaster. All of them together is the Eclosion.

## Hooks

- A well that goes quiet whenever someone sings near it.
