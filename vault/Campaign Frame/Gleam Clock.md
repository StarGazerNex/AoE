Your notes say each well has a slow clock the GM can advance, and that the game system is still open. This note proposes a clock and a light, system-agnostic exposure track.

## The Well Clock
Every well has **12 Tide Marks**. Tick it when:
- The party is away or inactive for a notable stretch (about 1 mark per arc).
- The well is fed: crowds, a Giving, a boom season.
- The well is angered: badly sealed, burned, robbed.

Un-tick it (once, never below its starting mark) when the party quiets it: a Tide-song, a proper seal, a Hearth rite.

| Marks | What people see |
|---|---|
| 0-3 | A pool. Mild, helpful, the miracle. |
| 4-6 | A boom. A town becomes a wellcity. |
| 7-9 | Animals drift in. Grass thins. People stop sleeping. |
| 10-11 | Humming. The surface *leans* toward visitors. |
| 12 | The well opens. |

**Your Stage-3 question.** Suggested answer: no PC starts above 2. Two special cases:
- [[Harald]]'s Astroderm *is* Gleam held at the surface, so he does not climb the track. His risk is different: **Overflow**, when the skin cannot hold more.
- [[Nymestria]] was injected with "gleamblood" at the Sacrifice. She starts at 2, but the seed did not take, and she is the only person who knows why.

**Ancestry** *(suggested)*: Fungrils climb slowly and feel everything; Faerie climb fast and cheerfully; Clank hybrids like Luster do not climb through the arm, but the arm climbs.
