The Closing is the ritual that can end the Eclosion. It is written in the third folio of [[Lucens Liquidus]] and needs **six roles**. The six player characters were not chosen. They are the six people who ended up holding each role.

## The Six Roles
| Role | Who | What it does | Why them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lantern** | [[Harald]] | Holds Gleam at the surface and draws the flood into the skin | Astroderm |
| **Hearth** | [[Mog'el]] | Keeps the measured rite: smoke shared, purpose named, vision spoken | [[Smoke-Bound]] |
| **Watch** | [[Titania]] | Guards the circle and calls the taken by name | The night watch; [[Allium]]'s legacy |
| **Voice** | [[Luster]] | Sings what the Vael cannot copy | Untainted song, Tide-song, her own words |
| **Edge** | [[Nymestria]] | Severs a seed from its host without killing the host | A killer who learned to cut with care |
| **Anchor** | [[CIX]] | Stands in the lake and refuses the Hatchling's place | The vessel who said no |

## The Rule of the Hearth
From the Smoke-Bound: *the one who smokes names what they are smoking for.* In the Closing each person names **what they are doing it for**, aloud, before their role begins. Each name must be someone or something real. The Vael cannot copy a reason.

## The Price
Every role costs something and the player chooses what to pay.

| Role | Cost to hold it fully | If they hold back |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Astroderm spreads over Harald for good | Gleam flows back in; hosts do not wake |
| Hearth | Mog'el smokes the last rite and never again | The Hearth is a candle, not a fire |
| Watch | Tia lets [[Allium]] go | The circle breaks; hosts are retaken |
| Voice | Luster gives up the arm, or the brightness of her voice | The Chorus is quieted, not silenced |
| Edge | Nymestria cuts herself free of her own seed | Severed hosts die with their seeds |
| Anchor | CIX stays as the lake's sleeper, or leaves for good | The lake stays open |

## Partial Closings
- **Five of six:** the Eclosion is delayed one phase.
- **Four of six:** wells quiet but regrow; the Dry's seals become permanent work.
- **Three or fewer:** the Eclosion happens, flawed. See [[Endings]].
