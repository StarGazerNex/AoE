## Conventions
- New and expanded notes carry `origin: new` or `origin: expanded` in frontmatter, so a Bases view or search can show everything added.
- Where a new note answers one of your Open Questions, the answer sits under **Proposed answers** and your question stays untouched.
- `*(suggested)*` is your convention and is used the same way.
- GM secrets are always under a `GM only` callout.
