A century-old tome that weighs at least eleven pounds (five kilograms) and looks like a notebook. Worn leather cover, metallic fittings at each corner. See [[Harald]].

## Common Knowledge
Left on [[Harald]]'s desk at [[Plenilunium]] by a teacher. Nobody else has been able to read it.

## What It Is *(suggested)*
Its pages are beaten Gleam-leaf, which is why it is heavy. It was written by **Sael Moranthe**, the first [[Moonarch Superior]], about 60 AG, before she Converged. Only someone with Astroderm *and* Somniorum dream-magic can read it. It has three folios.

| Folio | Appears | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **I. The Vision** | Act I | The "drooling" vision. The Gleam's intention. |
| **II. The Ledger of Wells** | Act II | Every well and its Tide Mark; the Eclosion date. |
| **III. The Closing** | Act III | The six roles. See [[The Closing]]. |

## GM Truth
> [!warning] GM only
Harald's vision in the tome was not prophecy. It was the *intention* of the Gleam already inside him, shown through a book that Sael made to carry it. She wrote it as a warning. The [[Quiet Lamp]] left it for him.
